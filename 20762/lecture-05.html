期权基础定义与分类 (Options)

  • 期权类别 (Option class)：
    • 看涨期权 (Call)：买入标的资产的权利
    • 看跌期权 (Put)：卖出标的资产的权利
  • 期权行权类型 (Option types)：
    • 欧式期权 (European options)：仅能在到期日当天行权
    • 美式期权 (American options)：可在到期日或到期日之前的任何交易日行权
  • 期权头寸 (Option positions)：
    • 多头 (Long)：买方支付期权费，享有以有限下行风险博取杠杆收益的权利。
      • 买入期权并支付期权费（价格）• 享有权利，无履约义务
    • 空头 (Short)：卖方（立权人）收取期权费，但承担在买方行权时必须按约定履约的义务。
      • 卖出（签发）期权并收取期权费（价格）• 承担履约义务，无主动选择权

Options

  • Option class:
    • Call: Option to buy an underlying asset
    • Put: Option to sell an underlying asset
  • Option types:
    • European options: Can only be exercised at maturity
    • American options: Can be exercised at any time before or at maturity
  • Option positions:
    • Long: The buyer has the opportunity to earn a leveraged profit with limited risk, for which they pay a premium.
      • Buys option and pays premium (price) • Has a right
    • Short: The writer has the opportunity to earn premium income, but has the obligation to meet the claim of the buyer when exercised.
      • Sells (writes) option and receives premium (price) • Has an obligation

Options Positions

  • Long call: Buyer has right to buy underlying at strike \( K \):
    \(\text{Payoff} = \max(S_T - K, 0)\)
  • Short call: Writer has obligation to sell underlying at strike \( K \):
    \(\text{Payoff} = -\max(S_T - K, 0) = \min(K - S_T, 0)\)
  • Long put: Buyer has right to sell underlying at strike \( K \):
    \(\text{Payoff} = \max(K - S_T, 0)\)
  • Short put: Writer has obligation to buy underlying at strike \( K \):
    \(\text{Payoff} = -\max(K - S_T, 0) = \min(S_T - K, 0)\)

核心术语：内在价值与时间价值

  • 内在价值 (Intrinsic value)：假定期权立即按标的资产当前价格行权所能获得的理论收益：
    • 看涨期权内在价值：\( \max(S_t - K, 0) \)
    • 看跌期权内在价值：\( \max(K - S_t, 0) \)
  • 时间价值 (Time value)：期权当前市场价格超出其内在价值的溢价差额：
    \(\text{期权价格 (Premium)} = \text{内在价值 (Intrinsic Value)} + \text{时间价值 (Time Value)}\)
    • 为什么投资者愿意支付高于内在价值的溢价？因为期权赋予了持有者在未来股价发生有利变动时赚取巨额收益的可能，同时严格锁定了最大潜在下行亏损。
  • 计算案例：6 月份某股票市场价格为 $43。8 月到期、执行价 $40 的看涨期权内在价值为 \( \$43 - \$40 = \$3 \)。若当前市场期权费报价为 $5，则其时间价值为 \( \$5 - \$3 = \$2 \)。

Terminology

  • Intrinsic value: The payoff if the option was exercised at the current price of the underlying asset:
    • Call intrinsic value: \( \max(S_t - K, 0) \)
    • Put intrinsic value: \( \max(K - S_t, 0) \)
  • Time value: The excess of the option's current market price above its intrinsic value:
    \(\text{Option Premium} = \text{Intrinsic Value} + \text{Time Value}\)
    • Why pay more than intrinsic value? It provides the possibility of making substantial profit by exercising under favourable future price movements with strictly limited downside.
  • Example: In June, a stock trades at $43. The August 40 call then has an intrinsic value of \( \$43 - \$40 = \$3 \). Suppose the option premium is quoted at $5. Its time value is thus \( \$5 - \$3 = \$2 \).

Terminology: Moneyness

  • At-the-money (ATM):
    • An option where the current stock price equals strike: \( S_t = K \)
    • Its intrinsic value is zero: \( \max(S_t - K, 0) = 0 \)
  • In-the-money (ITM):
    • An option with positive intrinsic value:
    • \( S_t > K \), if it is a call
    • \( S_t < K \), if it is a put
  • Out-of-the-money (OTM):
    • An option with zero intrinsic value that would not be exercised today:
    • \( S_t < K \), if it is a call
    • \( S_t > K \), if it is a put

期权价值分解与非线性时间价值衰减

期权的总市场价格由内在价值（立即行权价值）与时间价值（对未来波动的投机性溢价）两部分构成。时间价值衰减 (\(\theta\)) 呈现严格的非线性与指数加速特征，在距到期最后 30 天内衰减速度急剧加快。

执行价格 (\(K\)) 期权值度 (\(S_0 = \$125\)) 期权总价格 内在价值 时间价值 到期演化行为
$120 看涨期权 实值 (ITM) $8.50 \( 125 - 120 = \mathbf{\$5.00} \) $3.50 保留 \( \max(S_T - 120, 0) \)；时间价值衰减归零
$125 看涨期权 平值 (ATM) $5.00 \( \max(125 - 125, 0) = \mathbf{\$0.00} \) $5.00 (100%) 拥有最高的时间价值与 Gamma；对波动率变化最敏感
$130 看涨期权 虚值 (OTM) $2.00 \( \mathbf{\$0.00} \) $2.00 (100%) 若 \( S_T \le 130 \)，到期价值将 100% 归零作废
  • 虚值期权到期归宿：所有虚值期权内在价值均为零。若到期时未能进入实值区间，全部期权费将归零蒸发。

Decomposing Option Value & Non-Linear Time Decay

Core Principle: An option's total market price consists of two distinct components: Intrinsic Value (immediate exercise value) and Time Value (the speculative premium for potential future stock movements). Time decay (\(\theta\)) is strictly non-linear and exponential, accelerating rapidly during the final 30 days before expiration.

Strike (\(K\)) Moneyness (\(S_0 = \$125\)) Total Premium Intrinsic Value Time Value Behavior at Expiration
$120 Call In-The-Money (ITM) $8.50 \( 125 - 120 = \mathbf{\$5.00} \) $3.50 Retains \( \max(S_T - 120, 0) \); time value decays to $0
$125 Call At-The-Money (ATM) $5.00 \( \max(125 - 125, 0) = \mathbf{\$0.00} \) $5.00 (100%) Highest time value / gamma; greatest sensitivity to volatility
$130 Call Out-of-The-Money (OTM) $2.00 \( \mathbf{\$0.00} \) $2.00 (100%) Expires completely worthless if \( S_T \le 130 \)
  • OTM Expiration Reality: All out-of-the-money options have zero intrinsic value. If they remain OTM at maturity, they expire 100% worthless.
  • Why pay for OTM? Traders buy OTM options solely for leverage; small stock movements can produce large percentage gains in the option's time value prior to expiry.

红利与股票拆分对期权条款的调整

  • 公司行为导致的期权条款调整规则：
    • 考虑标的为某股票的 \( N \) 份执行价格为 \( K \) 的期权。
    • 现金红利 (Cash dividends)：交易所交易期权对上市公司发放的普通现金红利不作任何条款调整。
    • 股票拆分 (Stock splits)：当公司执行 \( n \)-拆-\( m \)（即原 \( m \) 股拆细为 \( n \) 股）时：
      • 执行价格相应下调为 \( \frac{m}{n} K \)
      • 每份合约涵盖的股票数量调整为 \( \frac{n}{m} N \)
    • 送红股（股票股利）：与股票拆分遵循完全相同的调整比例处理。
  • 典型案例演示：假设某看涨期权涵盖以每股 $20 买入 100 股标的股票。

Dividends and Stock Splits

  • Option adjustments for corporate actions:
    • Consider \( N \) options with strike \( K \).

    • Stock splits: When there is an \( n \)-for-\( m \) stock split:

价格影响因素的经济学机制剖析

  • 现货价格与执行价格：
    • 看涨期权内在价值：\( S_t - K \)（随 \(S_0\) 上涨而增加，随 \(K\) 上涨而减少）
    • 看跌期权内在价值：\( K - S_t \)（随 \(S_0\) 上涨而减少，随 \(K\) 上涨而增加）
  • 期限时间：
    • 欧式期权：仅能在到期日行权一次。若延长期限内有大额红利派发，可能压低到期股票价格，导致其欧式看涨价格下降（故方向不确定 "?"）。
    • 美式期权：拥有随时行权的完全自主权 → 期限越长，选择权越多，价值必定越高。
  • 波动率：
    • 期权具有“下行亏损有限、上行收益无限”的凸性收益特征：持有者享受有利波动的利润，不利波动时直接放弃行权。
    • 波动率越高，标的资产出现大幅极端有利波动的概率越大，同时推高看涨与看跌期权价值。
  • 无风险利率：
    • 利率上升提高标的资产的预期增长漂移率，同时降低未来行权支付执行价 \(K\) 的贴现现值。
    • 当 \(r\) 上升时：看涨期权价格上升，看跌期权价格下降。
  • 预期红利：除息日股价会因红利剥离而下跌对应金额，因此红利增加降低看涨价格、推高看跌价格。

How Parameters Affect Option Prices

  • Spot and Strike:
    • Call intrinsic value: \( S_t - K \) (increases with \(S_0\), decreases with \(K\))
    • Put intrinsic value: \( K - S_t \) (decreases with \(S_0\), increases with \(K\))
  • Time:
    • European: only one opportunity to exercise at maturity.
    • American: More flexibility on when to exercise → longer life always adds value.
    • Dividend expected during the extended period? May reduce European call value.
  • Volatility:
    • Options eliminate downside risk: owner benefits from upside, walks away on downside.
    • Higher volatility increases the probability of large favorable outcomes, raising both calls and puts.
  • Interest rates:
    • Higher risk-free rate increases expected stock drift and reduces the PV of strike \(K\).
    • When \(r\) increases: call price increases, put price decreases.
  • Dividends:
    • Stock price drops on ex-dividend date by the dividend amount.
    • As dividend \(D\) increases: call price decreases, put price increases.

Put-Call Parity

  • Consider the following two portfolios:
    • Portfolio A: One European call option on a stock plus the PV of the strike price in cash: \( c + K e^{-rT} \)
    • Portfolio B: One European put option on the stock plus one share of stock: \( p + S_0 \)
  • Payoff at maturity (\(T\)):
    • Portfolio A: \( \max(S_T - K, 0) + K = \max(S_T, K) \)
    • Portfolio B: \( \max(K - S_T, 0) + S_T = \max(S_T, K) \)
  • No-Arbitrage Relationship: Both portfolios yield identical payoffs under all possible states of the world at maturity. Therefore, they must have the same market value today:
    \( c + K e^{-rT} = p + S_0 \)
  • For a dividend-paying stock (with \(D = \text{PV of dividends}\)):
    \( c + K e^{-rT} = p + S_0 - D \)

美式期权核心性质 (American Options)

  • 与欧式期权的价值关系：美式期权价值必定不低于对应的欧式期权，因为美式期权包含了欧式期权的全部行权权利外加提前行权的灵活性：
    \[ C \ge c \quad \text{且} \quad P \ge p \]
  • 提前行权概率：通常情况下，美式期权存在正的提前行权概率溢价。
  • 最关键的例外定理：对于不支付红利的股票，美式看涨期权提前行权永远不是最优选择。因此：
    \[ C = c \]
    • 资金时间价值：推迟行权可将支付执行价所需的资金 \(K\) 保留在银行持续赚取无风险利息。
    • 无红利损失：由于股票不分红，推迟行权不会遭受任何股利流失。
    • 下行保险保护：持有看涨期权提供了防范股价跌破执行价的保险功能。提前行权过早将受保护的期权转化为了承担完全下跌风险的现货股票。

American Options

  • Value relation to European options: An American option is worth at least as much as the corresponding European option because it includes all European exercise rights plus early exercise:
    \( C \ge c \quad \text{and} \quad P \ge p \)
  • Early exercise probability: Usually there is some positive probability that an American option will be exercised early.
  • The Major Exception: An American call on a non-dividend paying stock is never optimal to exercise early. Therefore:
    \( C = c \)
    • Time value of money: Delaying exercise keeps the strike price \(K\) earning interest in the bank.
    • No dividend sacrifice: No income is foregone, as the stock pays no dividend.
    • Downside protection: Holding the call option provides insurance against the stock price falling below the strike. Exercising early converts an insured option into risky stock.

    • If you do not want to hold the stock: Do NOT exercise! Sell the option in the market.

Should Puts Be Exercised Early?

  • Time value of money favor early exercise: Exercising a put realizes the cash strike price \(K\) immediately, which can be invested to earn interest.
  • Trade-off with downside protection:
    • Holding the put option provides downside protection against the stock falling below \(K\).
    • However, if the put is deep in the money (stock price is very close to zero), the stock cannot fall much further. The insurance value is near zero!
    • It becomes far more attractive to receive \(K\) immediately and earn interest than to hold an option with no remaining upside.
  • Example: Consider an American put with:
    \( S_0 = \$10, \quad T = 0.25, \quad K = \$50, \quad r = 10\% \)
    • Should you exercise immediately? YES!
    • Exercising yields \( K - S_0 = \$50 - \$10 = \mathbf{\$40} \) today.
    • Investing $40 at 10% for 3 months yields interest of \( 40(e^{0.025} - 1) \approx \mathbf{\$1.01} \).
    • Even if the stock price drops to $0, the extra payoff is only $10, which may be less than the interest earned.

提前行权边界：美式看跌期权 vs 看涨期权

看涨与看跌期权的核心非对称性

提前行权本质上是现金利息收入与下行保险保护价值之间的微观经济权衡。

美式看涨期权（无红利）
  • 提前行权绝非最优：\( C = c \)。
  • 行权需要提前支付现金 \( K \)，放弃资金在银行的利息收入 \( rK \)。
  • 股价理论上具有无限上涨空间；持有期权保留了免受跌破 \( K \) 损失的保护。
  • 支付红利情形：仅在除息日前夕，且预期红利现值超过推迟行权节省的利息损失 \( D > K(1 - e^{-r(T-t_d)}) \) 时，提前行权才可能成立。
美式看跌期权（深度实值）
  • 提前行权频繁为最优：\( P > p \)。
  • 行权可立即收取执行价现金 \( K \)，随即开始计收无风险利息 \( rK \)。
  • 股价下行有严格底线（最低为 $0）。当 \( S_t \to 0 \) 时，下行保险价值趋近于零，而资金利息收益确定无疑。
  • 临界行权边界 \( S^* \)：在每个时刻 \( t < T \)，均存在一个临界行权价格阈值 \( S^*(t) < K \)，股价一旦跌破该边界，立即行权便为严格最优决策。
实用交易法则：绝不要提前行权美式看涨期权（除非遇上除息日前夕的大额分红）——若欲平仓，应直接在市场上卖出该期权！对于深度实值的美式看跌期权，密切监控股价是否击穿临界边界 \( S^* \)，一旦击穿应立即行权锁定资金时间价值。

The Early Exercise Boundary: American Puts vs Calls

Core Asymmetry Between Calls and Puts

Early exercise is fundamentally an economic trade-off between interest earned on cash and the value of downside insurance.

American Calls (No Dividends)
  • Never optimal to exercise early: \( C = c \).
  • Exercising requires paying cash \( K \) early, forfeiting interest income \( rK \).
  • Stock price has unlimited upside; holding the call preserves insurance against a decline below \( K \).
  • With dividends: Early exercise can only be optimal immediately prior to an ex-dividend date if the dividend exceeds foregone interest: \( D > K(1 - e^{-r(T-t_d)}) \).
American Puts (Deep In-The-Money)
  • Frequently optimal to exercise early: \( P > p \).
  • Exercising receives cash \( K \) immediately, which starts earning risk-free interest \( rK \).
  • Stock price cannot fall below $0 (bounded below). As \( S_t \to 0 \), downside insurance becomes worthless while interest on \( K \) is guaranteed.
  • Critical Boundary \( S^* \): At each time \( t < T \), there exists a critical threshold \( S^*(t) < K \) below which immediate exercise is strictly optimal.
Practical Trading Rule: If you hold an American call on a non-dividend stock and wish to exit, never exercise early — sell it in the secondary market to capture both intrinsic value and unexpired time value!
