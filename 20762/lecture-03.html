投资性资产与消费性资产

  • 投资性资产 (Investment Assets)： 由大量投资者完全出于投资目的而持有的资产。
    • 典型代表：股票、债券、黄金、白银。
  • 消费性资产 (Consumption Assets)： 主要用于工业生产制造原料或日常消费而持有的商品。
    • 典型代表：农产品（玉米、小麦）、铜、原油等大宗工业品。
  • 资产定价的核心区别：
    • 运用无套利证明可以推导确定投资性资产的精确远期与期货价格。
    • 套利机制仅能为消费性资产确定一个理论价格上限（反向套利受阻）。

Consumption vs Investment Assets

  • Investment assets are held by a significant number of investors purely for investment purposes.
    • Examples: stocks, bonds, gold, silver.
  • Consumption assets are held primarily for consumption or manufacturing input.
    • Examples: agricultural goods (corn, wheat), copper, crude oil.
  • Crucial Difference in Pricing:
    • Arbitrage arguments can be used to determine exact forward and futures prices of investment assets.
    • Arbitrage arguments provide only an upper bound for consumption assets:
      • Ownership of the physical commodity provides benefits – convenience yield.
      • Short selling of physical commodities is not always possible.
      • Some commodities cannot be stored easily or at all (e.g., electricity).

卖空机制 (Short Selling)

  • 卖空是指投资者卖出自己当前并不拥有的证券资产：
    • 你的经纪人从其他客户账户中借入该证券，并在即期现货市场上正常抛售。
    • 在未来某一时间，你必须从市场上买回该证券（买入平仓 Short Covering），以归还给最初出借股票的客户账户。
    • 你必须向出借方足额补偿在借出期间所派发的一切股息红利及其他资本分红。
    • 你必须在经纪商处维持充足的保证金账户以防范违约风险。
  • 为何进行卖空？
    • 投机策略： 预期标的资产价格未来将下跌，从中博取跌幅利润。
    • 套利交易： 当期货价格相对现货被严重低估时，执行反向现货-期货套利 (Reverse Cash-and-Carry)。

Short Selling

  • Short selling involves selling securities you do not currently own:
    • Your broker borrows the securities from another client and sells them in the spot market in the usual way.
    • At some future stage, you must buy the securities back (short covering) so they can be replaced in the account of the original lender.
    • You must pay all dividends and other distributions that the owner of the borrowed securities would ordinarily have received.
    • You are required to maintain a margin account with the broker to protect against default risk.
  • Why short sell?
    • Speculation: Profit from an anticipated decline in the asset price.
    • Arbitrage: Exploit relative mispricing when the forward contract is underpriced (reverse cash-and-carry).

案例分析 – 利用卖空进行投机交易

  • 交易情境设定：
    • 4月份： 某投资者以 $120 卖空 500 股 IBM 股票。
      → 投资者的经纪商借入 500 股并在即期现货市场上以 $120 售出。
    • 5月份： IBM 公司派发每股 $1 的现金红利。
    • 7月份： 投资者在市场上以每股 $100 买回股票并进行平仓。
      → 经纪商以 $100 价格买入 500 股并归还原出借人。
  • 现金流最终核算：
    • 4月份卖空获得现金收入：\( 500 \times \$120 = +\$60,000 \)。
    • 5月份代为支付股利红利：\( 500 \times \$1 = -\$500 \)。
    • 7月份买回股票平仓支出：\( 500 \times \$100 = -\$50,000 \)。
    • 锁定投机净利润： \( \$60,000 - \$500 - \$50,000 = \mathbf{+\$9,500} \)。

Example – Speculation with Short Selling

  • Setup:
    • In April: An investor shorts 500 IBM shares at \$120.
      → The investor’s broker borrows 500 shares and sells them in the spot market for \$120.
    • In May: IBM pays a dividend of \$1 per share.
    • In July: The investor buys the shares back for \$100 and closes out the short position.
      → The broker buys 500 shares at \$100 to return to the original lender.
  • The Outcome:
    • The investor receives: \( 500 \times \$120 = +\$60,000 \) in April.
    • The investor must pay: \( 500 \times \$1 = -\$500 \) in May (dividend reimbursement).
    • The investor pays: \( 500 \times \$100 = -\$50,000 \) in July to close out.
    • Net Gain (ignoring time value of money): \[ 500 \times (\$120 - \$1 - \$100) = 500 \times \$19 = \mathbf{\$9,500} \]

远期定价与符号记法

  • \( S_0 \)：资产在时刻 \( t = 0 \) 的即期现货价格。
  • \( S_t \)：资产在时刻 \( t \) 的即期现货价格。
  • \( F_0 \)：在时刻 \( t = 0 \) 商定的远期价格：
    • 指在 \( t=0 \) 签订的、在到期日 \( T \) 进行交割的远期合约中所约定的交割价格。
  • \( F_t \)：在时刻 \( t \) 市场上新签署的远期价格：
    • 指在时刻 \( t \) 市场上达成的新远期合约中约定的到期 \( T \) 的交割价格。
  • \( f_0 \)：远期合约在初始时刻 (\( t = 0 \)) 的合约价值。恒有 \( f_0 = 0 \)。
  • \( f_t \)：最初在 \( t=0 \) 签订、在 \( T \) 交割的远期合约在存续期时刻 \( t \) 的合约市场价值。
    • 核心区别： \( F_t \) 是新合约的价格，而 \( f_t \) 是存续期老合约本身的净现值！
  • \( r \)：具有到期期限 \( T \) 的年化无风险利率（采用连续复利形式）。

Forward Price – Notation

  • \( S_0 \): spot price of the asset at time \( t = 0 \).
  • \( S_t \): spot price of the asset at time \( t \).
  • \( F_0 \): forward price at time \( t = 0 \):
    • The delivery price agreed in a contract negotiated at \( t=0 \) for delivery at maturity \( T \).
  • \( F_t \): forward price at time \( t \):
    • The delivery price in new forward contracts negotiated at time \( t \) for delivery at \( T \).
  • \( f_0 \): value of the forward contract at initiation (\( t = 0 \)). Always \( f_0 = 0 \).
  • \( f_t \): value at time \( t \) of a forward contract originally negotiated at \( t=0 \) for delivery at \( T \).
    • Note: \( F_t \) (price) and \( f_t \) (value) are fundamentally different concepts!
  • \( r \): risk-free interest rate per annum with continuous compounding.

远期价格 – 无收益投资性资产

设市场实际远期报价为 \( F^* \)。与理论无套利远期价格对比：

  • 若 \( F^* > S_0 e^{rT} \)（远期价格被高估）：
    • 套利者执行正向现货-期货套利 (Cash-and-Carry Arbitrage)： 按利率 \( r \) 借入 \( S_0 \)，以 \( S_0 \) 买入现货资产，并按 \( F^* \) 签订远期卖出合约。 在到期时刻 \( T \)，交付资产收取 \( F^* \)，偿还贷款本息 \( S_0 e^{rT} \)。 锁定确定性无风险净利润 = \( F^* - S_0 e^{rT} > 0 \)。
  • 若 \( F^* < S_0 e^{rT} \)（远期价格被低估）：
    • 套利者执行反向现货-期货套利 (Reverse Cash-and-Carry Arbitrage)： 卖空现货资产获得 \( S_0 \)，将款项按 \( r \) 贷出/存入银行，并按 \( F^* \) 买入远期多头。 在到期日收取投资本息 \( S_0 e^{rT} \)，支付 \( F^* \) 接受远期交割并归还借股。 锁定确定性无风险净利润 = \( S_0 e^{rT} - F^* > 0 \)。
  • 理论无套利远期价格公式：
    \[ F_0 = S_0 e^{rT} \]

Forward Price – Investment Asset with No Income

Let the market-quoted forward price be \( F^* \). Compare with theoretical no-arbitrage price:

  • If \( F^* > S_0 e^{rT} \) (Forward Overpriced):
    • Arbitrageur executes Cash-and-Carry Arbitrage: Borrow \( S_0 \) at rate \( r \), buy the asset at \( S_0 \), and sell forward at \( F^* \). At \( T \), deliver the asset for \( F^* \), repay loan \( S_0 e^{rT} \). Profit locked in = \( F^* - S_0 e^{rT} > 0 \).
  • If \( F^* < S_0 e^{rT} \) (Forward Underpriced):
    • Arbitrageur executes Reverse Cash-and-Carry Arbitrage: Short sell the asset at \( S_0 \), invest proceeds in risk-free deposit at \( r \), and buy forward at \( F^* \). At \( T \), deposit matures to \( S_0 e^{rT} \), buy asset via forward at \( F^* \) to close short. Profit locked in = \( S_0 e^{rT} - F^* > 0 \).
  • Theoretical Forward Price:
    \[ F_0 = S_0 e^{rT} \quad \text{or at time } t: \quad F_t = S_t e^{r(T-t)} \]
    Examples: Non-dividend-paying stocks and zero-coupon bonds.

远期价格 – 支付已知现金收益的资产

假定 \( I \) 为合约存续期内将收到的所有已知现金收益的现值 (Present Value)：

  • 若 \( F^* > (S_0 - I) e^{rT} \)（远期价格被高估）：
    • 套利者借入资金 \( S_0 \) 买入现货资产，并做空卖出远期合约。
    • 存续期内收到的红利按利率 \( r \) 滚动投资，到期用于冲抵偿还贷款本息。
  • 若 \( F^* < (S_0 - I) e^{rT} \)（远期价格被低估）：
    • 套利者以 \( S_0 \) 卖空现货资产，将 \( (S_0 - I) \) 的资金按 \( r \) 贷出，并做多买入远期合约。
    • 借股期间需代为垫付的红利由留存备付资金 \( I \) 及其利息精准支付。
  • 理论无套利定价公式：
    \[ F_0 = (S_0 - I) e^{rT} \]

Forward Price – Asset Paying Known Dollar Income

Suppose \( I \) is the present value of all known cash income received during the life of the contract:

  • If \( F^* > (S_0 - I) e^{rT} \) (Overpriced):
    • Arbitrageur borrows \( S_0 \) to buy the asset and enters a short forward contract.
    • The income received during the contract life is invested at rate \( r \) and helps repay the loan at \( T \).
  • If \( F^* < (S_0 - I) e^{rT} \) (Underpriced):
    • Arbitrageur short sells the asset for \( S_0 \), deposits amount \( (S_0 - I) \) at \( r \), and enters a long forward contract.
    • Income payments on the borrowed asset are paid out of the remaining short proceeds.
  • Theoretical Forward Price:
    \[ F_0 = (S_0 - I) e^{rT} \quad \text{and at time } t: \quad F_t = (S_t - I_t) e^{r(T-t)} \]
    Examples: Dividend-paying stocks with known dividend amounts and coupon-bearing bonds.

案例分析 – 支付红利股票的套利机会

  • 市场基础数据：
    • 股票即期现货价格：\( S_0 = \$50 \)。
    • 10 个月远期市场报价：\( F^* = \$52 \)。
    • 连续复利年化无风险利率：所有期限均为 \( r = 8\% \) p.a.。
    • 预期现金红利：在 3 个月、6 个月及 9 个月后各派发每股 \( \$0.75 \)。
  • 是否存在无套利机会？
    • 步骤 1：计算红利现金流现值 (\(I\))： \[ I = 0.75 e^{-0.08 \times 3/12} + 0.75 e^{-0.08 \times 6/12} + 0.75 e^{-0.08 \times 9/12} \] \[ I = 0.75 (0.98020 + 0.96079 + 0.94176) = 0.75 \times 2.88275 = \mathbf{\$2.162} \]
    • 步骤 2：计算理论无套利远期价格 (\(F_0\))： \[ F_0 = (50 - 2.162) e^{0.08 \times 10/12} = 47.838 \times 1.06894 = \mathbf{\$51.14} \]
    • 判定： 由于市场报价 \( F^* = \$52 > \$51.14 \)，远期价格被高估 → 执行正向现货-期货套利！

Example – Arbitrage with Dividend-Paying Stock

  • Market Setup:
    • Spot price of the stock: \( S_0 = \$50 \).
    • Quoted 10-month forward price: \( F^* = \$52 \).
    • Risk-free interest rate: \( r = 8\% \) p.a. (continuously compounded) for all maturities.
    • Expected dividends: \( \$0.75 \) per share in 3 months, 6 months, and 9 months.
  • Is there an arbitrage opportunity?
    • Step 1: Calculate PV of dividends (\(I\)): \[ I = 0.75 e^{-0.08 \times 3/12} + 0.75 e^{-0.08 \times 6/12} + 0.75 e^{-0.08 \times 9/12} \] \[ I = 0.75 (0.98020 + 0.96079 + 0.94176) = 0.75 \times 2.88275 = \mathbf{\$2.162} \]
    • Step 2: Calculate theoretical forward price: \[ F_0 = (S_0 - I) e^{rT} = (50 - 2.162) e^{0.08 \times 10/12} = 47.838 \times 1.06894 = \mathbf{\$51.14} \]
    • Conclusion: Quoted price \( F^* = \$52 > F_0 = \$51.14 \).
      The forward contract is overpriced by \( \$52 - \$51.14 = \mathbf{\$0.86} \).

Forward Price – Asset Providing Known Yield

Suppose \( q \) is the average continuous dividend yield of the asset during the contract life:

\[ F_0 = S_0 e^{(r - q)T} \quad \text{and at time } t: \quad F_t = S_t e^{(r - q)(T - t)} \]
  • Example – Stock Index:
    • A stock index represents a portfolio paying dividends throughout the year.
    • Dividends can be modeled as a continuous yield \( q \).
  • Crucial Condition for Index Futures:
    • For this formula to hold, the index must represent an investment asset.
    • In other words, changes in the index must correspond to changes in the value of an actually tradable portfolio of securities.
    • Counter-example: The Nikkei 225 index viewed purely as a dollar number is not a tradable investment asset (it combines Japanese yen prices directly into a non-currency portfolio).

远期合约的存续期价值评估

考虑一份在时刻 \( 0 \) 订立、约定交割价格为 \( F_0 \)、到期日为 \( T \) 的远期合约：

  • 在存续期时刻 \( t \)，多头远期合约的市场价值为： \[ f_t = (F_t - F_0) e^{-r(T - t)} \]
  • 在存续期时刻 \( t \)，空头远期合约的市场价值为： \[ f_t = (F_0 - F_t) e^{-r(T - t)} \]
  • 其实质反映了在时刻 \( t \) 提前对冲平仓该合约所能锁定的未来盈亏的贴现现值。
  • 在合约成立初始时刻 (\(t = 0\))： \( f_0 = 0 \)，因为初始交割价格恰好设定为理论远期价格 \( F_0 \)。

Valuing Forward Contracts

Consider a forward contract negotiated at time \( 0 \) with delivery price \( F_0 \) at maturity \( T \):

  • The value of a long position at time \( t \) is: \[ f_t = (F_t - F_0) e^{-r(T - t)} \]
  • The value of a short position at time \( t \) is: \[ f_t = (F_0 - F_t) e^{-r(T - t)} \]
  • It reflects the present value of the gain or loss from closing out the position at time \( t \).
  • At initiation (\(t = 0\)): \( f_0 = 0 \) because the delivery price is set to \( F_0 \).
  • Worked Example: \( S_0 = \$400 \), \( T = 6 \) months (0.5 yr), \( r = 5\% \).
    • At \( t=0 \): \( F_0 = 400 e^{0.05 \times 0.5} = \mathbf{\$410.13} \), and \( f_0 = 0 \).
    • After 2 months (\( t = 2/12 \), remaining \( \tau = 4/12 \)), stock rises to \( S_t = \$450 \): \[ F_t = 450 e^{0.05 \times (4/12)} = \mathbf{\$457.56} \] \[ f_t = (457.56 - 410.13) e^{-0.05 \times (4/12)} = 47.43 \times 0.98347 = \mathbf{\$46.65} \]

远期价格与期货价格的比较

  • 当利率恒定或完全可预测时：
    • 若市场利率保持常数，或者是时间的确定性已知函数，远期价格与期货价格在理论上完全等价一致：\( F_0 = \text{期货}_0 \)。
  • 当利率随机不确定且存在每日逐日盯市结算时：
    • 标的资产价格与利率呈正相关： 当标的资产价格 \( S \) 上涨时，期货多头持有者立即收到每日现金结算盈利，该现金流可在更高的利率环境下再投资。因此，期货价格 > 远期价格。
    • 标的资产价格与利率呈负相关： 远期价格 > 期货价格。
    • 但在实际绝大多数市场合约中，二者的理论差异微乎其微，在实际交易分析中通常视为等同。

Forward vs Futures Prices

  • Constant or Predictable Interest Rates:
    • When interest rates are constant or known functions of time, forward prices and futures prices are theoretically identical: \( F_0 = \text{Futures}_0 \).
  • Uncertain Interest Rates & Daily Settlement:
    • Positive correlation between asset price and interest rates: As \( S \) rises, long futures holders receive daily cash gains invested at higher interest rates. Thus, futures price > forward price.
    • Negative correlation: Forward price > futures price.
    • In practice, for short/medium maturities, these differences are very small → assume \( \text{Futures Price} = \text{Forward Price} \).
  • Is the Value of a Forward Contract the Same as Futures? – NO!
    • Futures: Profit/loss is realized daily through marking to market. Value is reset to zero daily.
    • Forward: All profit/loss is locked until maturity \( T \). Value at \( t \) is the present value of the change: \( f_t = (F_t - F_0) e^{-r(T-t)} \).

股指期货与股指套利 (Index Arbitrage)

Futures on Stock Indices – Index Arbitrage

  • When \( F^* > S_0 e^{(r - q)T} \) (Futures Overpriced):
    • Arbitrageur buys the underlying index stocks and enters a short position in index futures contracts (Cash-and-carry).
    • Often done by institutions with low transaction costs or corporations holding short-term money market funds.
  • When \( F^* < S_0 e^{(r - q)T} \) (Futures Underpriced):
    • Arbitrageur enters a long position in index futures contracts and sells (or short sells) the underlying stocks (Reverse cash-and-carry).
    • Frequently executed by large pension funds that can sell physical shares and invest in cash.
  • Execution & Market Realities:
    • Index arbitrage requires simultaneous trades in hundreds of stocks and index futures; automated program trading is standard.
    • Black Monday (19 October 1987): Massive order imbalances caused computer trading delays. Cash and futures decoupled, breaking the theoretical parity relationship.

Reverse Cash-and-Carry Arbitrage Trade Table

Problem Setup: S&P 200 index \( S_0 = 400 \), \( r = 10\% \), dividend yield \( q = 4\% \) (continuous). 4-month futures quoted at \( F^* = 405 \) (\( T = 4/12 = 1/3 \)).
Theoretical Price: \( F_0 = 400 e^{(0.10 - 0.04) \times (1/3)} = 400 e^{0.02} = \mathbf{408.08} \).
Since quoted \( F^* = 405 < 408.08 \), futures are underpriced → execute Reverse Cash-and-Carry.

Leg / Action Cash Flow at \( t = 0 \) Cash Flow at \( T = 4 \) months
1. Long Futures Contract (buy at 405) \( \$0.00 \) \( +(S_T - 405) \)
2. Short Sell Index Portfolio at spot \( S_0 = 400 \) \( +\$400.00 \) \( -S_T \) (repurchase index to close short)
3. Reimburse Dividend Yield on shorted index \( \$0.00 \) \( -400 (e^{0.04 \times 1/3} - 1) = -400 (1.01342 - 1) = -\$5.38 \)
4. Invest Short Proceeds at risk-free rate \( r = 10\% \) \( -\$400.00 \) \( +400 e^{0.10 \times 1/3} = 400 \times 1.03390 = +\$413.56 \)
Net Cash Flows (Arbitrage Profit) \( \mathbf{\$0.00} \) \( +413.56 - 405 - 5.38 + (S_T - S_T) = \mathbf{+\$3.08} \)

Core Principle: The terminal index price \( S_T \) cancels out completely! The locked-in profit at maturity is exactly the mispricing: \( F_0 - F^* = 408.08 - 405 = \mathbf{\$3.08} \) per contract unit (or \( \$3.08 e^{-0.10 \times 1/3} = \mathbf{\$2.98} \) in present value terms), with zero initial capital and zero market risk.

  • Notation:
    • \( S_0 \): spot exchange rate – price in domestic currency of 1 unit of foreign currency.
    • \( F_0 \): forward exchange rate – price in domestic currency of 1 unit of foreign currency.
    • \( r \): risk-free interest rate in domestic currency.
    • \( r_f \): risk-free interest rate in foreign currency.
  • Foreign Currency as an Income-Yielding Asset:
    • Holding foreign currency earns interest at the foreign rate \( r_f \).
    • Therefore, foreign currency is analogous to an investment asset paying a continuous dividend yield of \( q = r_f \):
    \[ F_0 = S_0 e^{(r - r_f)T} \quad \text{and at time } t: \quad F_t = S_t e^{(r - r_f)(T - t)} \]
  • Quotation Conventions:
    • Formula uses direct quotation (domestic currency per 1 unit of foreign currency).
    • In interbank FX markets, exchange rates are quoted as foreign currency per 1 USD, except for EUR, GBP, AUD, and NZD.

Futures and Forwards on Commodities

  • Investment Commodities (e.g., Gold, Silver):
    • Held for investment; storage costs can be treated as negative income.

持有成本模型 (Cost of Carry)

持有成本 (Cost of Carry) \( c \) 综合概括了资产持有过程中的资金融资成本、仓储保险成本以及所产生的收益流：

\[ c = \text{资金融资利息} + \text{仓储保险费用} - \text{持有期资产收益} \]
  • 对于无收益股票：\( c = r \)。
  • 对于提供红利率 \( q \) 的股票指数：\( c = r - q \)。
  • 对于具有外国利率 \( r_f \) 的外汇货币：\( c = r - r_f \)。
  • 对于具有比例仓储成本 \( u \) 的大宗商品：\( c = r + u \)。
  • 对于纯投资性资产： 期货定价公式可统一表达为：
    \[ F_0 = S_0 e^{cT} \]
    • 当 \( c > 0 \) 时，期货相对于现货产生升水：\( F_0 > S_0 \) – 升水市场 (Contango)。
    • 当 \( c < 0 \) 时，期货相对于现货产生贴水：\( F_0 < S_0 \) – 贴水市场 (Backwardation)。

Cost of Carry

The cost of carry \( c \) summarizes financing costs, storage costs, and income earned:

\[ c = \text{financing interest} + \text{storage costs} - \text{income earned} \]
  • \( c = r \) for a non-dividend paying stock.
  • \( c = r - q \) for a stock index providing dividend yield \( q \).
  • \( c = r - r_f \) for a currency with foreign interest rate \( r_f \).
  • \( c = r + u \) for a commodity with proportional storage cost \( u \).
  • For Investment Assets: \( F_0 = S_0 e^{cT} \).
    • Futures trade at a premium to spot: \( F_0 > S_0 \) whenever \( c > 0 \) – Contango.
  • For Consumption Assets: \( F_0 \le S_0 e^{cT} \).
    • Due to convenience yield, futures often trade lower than spot – Backwardation.

The Convenience Yield

  • Definition: The convenience yield \( y \) measures the economic benefits of holding the physical commodity rather than holding a futures contract:

Convenience Yield vs Storage & The 1955 Onion Corner

Unified Cost of Carry Model (David Harper)

\[ F_0 = S_0 e^{(r + u - y)T} \]

  • \( r \) (Financing): Increases forward price – capital tied up in holding inventory.
  • \( u \) (Storage & Spoilage): Increases forward price – cost of silos, tanks, refrigeration.
  • \( y \) (Convenience Yield): Reduces forward price – unmodeled operational value of physical stock.
  • Investment vs Consumption: For gold, \( y=0 \) (tight no-arbitrage bounds). For oil and corn, \( y > 0 \) and fluctuates with warehouse stock levels.

Historical Case: The 1955 Onion Squeeze

Labelled historical illustration, not a pricing proof:

  • The Corner: Vincent Kosuga and Sam Siegel bought 98% of all yellow onions in Chicago (over 30 million pounds), storing them in warehouses.
  • The Trap: With physical inventory cornered, spot prices spiked. But onions are perishable – storage cost \( u \) escalates and quality decays rapidly.
  • The Crash: They took huge short futures positions, then dumped millions of bags of rotting onions onto the spot market, driving prices from \( \$2.75 \) down to \( 10¢ \) a 50-lb bag!
  • Regulatory Legacy: Prompted the US Onion Futures Act of 1958, making onions the only commodity banned by federal law from futures trading.

Futures Prices and Expected Spot Prices

  • Market Conditions (Forward Curve Shape Today):
    • Contango: Futures price > current spot price (\( F_0 > S_0 \)).
    • Backwardation: Futures price < current spot price (\( F_0 < S_0 \)).
  • Market Expectations (Futures Price vs Expected Future Spot \( E[S_T] \)):
    • Normal Contango (\( F_0 > E[S_T] \)):
      • Keynes and Hicks argument: Hedgers hold net long positions (purchasers seeking price caps; expect losses on average).
      • Speculators hold short positions and earn an expected positive return.
      • Asset return is negatively correlated with the broader market (systematic risk is negative).
    • Normal Backwardation (\( F_0 < E[S_T] \)):
      • Keynes and Hicks argument: Hedgers hold net short positions (commodity producers seeking price floors; pay insurance premium).
      • Speculators hold long positions and expect to profit: \( E[S_T] - F_0 > 0 \).
      • Asset return is positively correlated with the broader market (positive systematic risk).

Contango & Backwardation vs Market Expectations

Concept Observed Curve Shape (Spot vs Futures) Expectations Theory (Futures vs \( E[S_T] \))
Contango Upward-sloping curve: \( F_0 > S_0 \).
Futures trade at a premium to current spot.
Driven by: Financing, storage costs, and high warehouse inventory (low convenience yield \( y \)). 		Normal Contango: \( F_0 > E[S_T] \).
Futures price is higher than expected future spot.
Long hedgers dominate; short speculators earn risk premium.
Backwardation Downward-sloping curve: \( F_0 < S_0 \).
Futures trade at a discount to current spot.
Driven by: Scarcity, tight inventories, and high convenience yield \( y \) to keep production running. 		Normal Backwardation: \( F_0 < E[S_T] \).
Futures price is lower than expected future spot.
Short hedgers dominate; long speculators earn risk premium.

Essential Distinctions from the CME Group Video:

  • Curve shape is dynamic: As market views of future spot prices change, the forward curve shifts between contango and backwardation.
  • Inventory Rule: Convenience yield is inversely related to warehouse inventory. High stocks → low \( y \) → contango. Low stocks → high \( y \) → backwardation.
  • No-Arbitrage Convergence: As delivery approaches (\( T \to 0 \)), futures price \( F_T \) must converge to spot \( S_T \); otherwise arbitrage exists.
