Delta (\(\Delta\))

  • Definition: Delta is the rate of change of the option price \(f\) with respect to the underlying asset price \(S\):
    \[ \Delta = \frac{\partial f}{\partial S} \]
  • European Calls and Puts on Non-Dividend Paying Assets:
    \[ \Delta_{\text{call}} = N(d_1) > 0, \quad \Delta_{\text{put}} = N(d_1) - 1 < 0 \]
  • Delta Ranges:
    • Call Delta lies between \(0\) and \(+1\) (\(\text{ATM} \approx 0.5\), deep ITM \(\to 1\), deep OTM \(\to 0\)).
    • Put Delta lies between \(-1\) and \(0\) (\(\text{ATM} \approx -0.5\), deep ITM \(\to -1\), deep OTM \(\to 0\)).

Delta 的核心性质

  • 斜率的几何含义： Delta 是期权价格关于标的资产价格 \(S\) 曲线上的切线斜率：
    \[ \text{切线斜率} = \Delta = \lim_{\Delta S \to 0} \frac{\Delta f}{\Delta S} \]
  • Delta 与到期时间的关系：
    • 当到期时间 \(T \to 0\) 时，Delta 曲线在执行价格 \(K\) 附近显著变陡：
      • 对于实值看涨期权（\(S > K\)）：\(\Delta \to 1.0\)
      • 对于虚值看涨期权（\(S < K\)）：\(\Delta \to 0.0\)
      • 对于平值看涨期权（\(S = K\)）：\(\Delta \approx 0.5\)，呈现阶跃函数的形态过渡。

Properties of Delta

  • Slope Interpretation: Delta is the slope of the curve plotting option price against the underlying asset price \(S\):
    \[ \text{Slope of tangent line} = \Delta = \lim_{\Delta S \to 0} \frac{\Delta f}{\Delta S} \]
  • Relation between Delta and Time to Maturity:
    • As time to maturity \(T \to 0\), the delta curve steepens around the strike \(K\):
      • For an ITM call (\(S > K\)): \(\Delta \to 1.0\)
      • For an OTM call (\(S < K\)): \(\Delta \to 0.0\)
      • For an ATM call (\(S = K\)): \(\Delta \approx 0.5\), transitioning like a step function.

Delta 的性质（一阶线性近似）

  • Delta 衡量期权价值对标的资产价格微小变动的敏感度：
    • 若标的价格 \(S\) 变动 \(\Delta S\)，则：
      • 看涨期权价值变动约为 \(\Delta_c \times \Delta S\)
      • 看跌期权价值变动约为 \(\Delta_p \times \Delta S = -|\Delta_p| \times \Delta S\)
  • 数值算例：
    股票价格从 $49.00 上涨至 $50.50（\(\Delta S = +\$1.50\)）。
    该股票看涨期权的 Delta 为 0.43。
    预计该看涨期权价值将上涨： \[ \Delta \times \Delta S = 0.43 \times \$1.50 = \mathbf{\$0.645} \]
  • 对于深度实值期权：\(\Delta_{\text{call}} \approx 1, \quad \Delta_{\text{put}} \approx -1\)。
  • 对于深度虚值期权：\(\Delta_{\text{call}} \approx 0, \quad \Delta_{\text{put}} \approx 0\)。

Properties of Delta (Approximation)

  • Delta measures the sensitivity of option value to small changes in underlying price:
    • If \(S\) increases by \(\Delta S\), then:
      • Call value changes by approximately \(\Delta_c \times \Delta S\)
      • Put value changes by approximately \(\Delta_p \times \Delta S = -|\Delta_p| \times \Delta S\)

    The stock price increases from $49.00 to $50.50 (\(\Delta S = +\$1.50\)).
    The delta of a call option on this stock is 0.43.
    The value of the option is expected to increase by: \[ \Delta \times \Delta S = 0.43 \times \$1.50 = \mathbf{\$0.645} \]
  • For deep ITM options: \(\Delta_{\text{call}} \approx 1, \quad \Delta_{\text{put}} \approx -1\).
  • For deep OTM options: \(\Delta_{\text{call}} \approx 0, \quad \Delta_{\text{put}} \approx 0\).

远期与期货合约的 Delta

  • 标的现货资产本身的 Delta 恒为 \(\mathbf{1}\)。
  • 支付连续红利率 \(q\)、期限为 \(T\) 的资产远期合约的 Delta：
    \[ \Delta_{\text{forward}} = e^{-qT} \quad (\text{若 } q = 0 \implies \Delta_{\text{forward}} = 1) \]
  • 支付连续红利率 \(q\)、期限为 \(T\) 的资产期货合约的 Delta：
    \[ \Delta_{\text{futures}} = e^{(r - q)T} \]

    • 股指期货：\(\Delta = e^{(r - q)T}\)
    • 外汇期货：\(\Delta = e^{(r - r_f)T}\)（\(r_f\) 为外国无风险利率）
    • 商品期货（黄金/白银）：\(\Delta = e^{rT}\)

Delta of Forward and Futures Contracts

  • The delta of the spot contract (underlying asset itself) is \(\mathbf{1}\).
  • The delta of a forward contract with maturity \(T\) on an asset paying continuous dividend yield \(q\):
    \[ \Delta_{\text{forward}} = e^{-qT} \quad (\text{if } q = 0 \implies \Delta_{\text{forward}} = 1) \]
  • The delta of a futures contract with maturity \(T\) on an asset paying continuous dividend yield \(q\):
    \[ \Delta_{\text{futures}} = e^{(r - q)T} \]
  • Examples:
    • Index Futures: \(\Delta = e^{(r - q)T}\)
    • Currency Futures: \(\Delta = e^{(r - r_f)T}\) (where \(r_f\) is foreign risk-free rate)
    • Commodity (Gold/Silver) Futures: \(\Delta = e^{rT}\)

Theta (\(\Theta\)) 时间衰减

  • 定义： Theta 是期权价格 \(f\) 相对于时间流逝 \(t\) 的变化率（时间价值损耗速率）：
    \[ \Theta = \frac{\partial f}{\partial t} \]
  • 支付连续红利率 \(q\) 资产的欧式看涨期权 Theta：
    \[ \Theta_c = -\frac{S_0 N'(d_1) \sigma e^{-qT}}{2\sqrt{T}} - r K e^{-rT} N(d_2) + q S_0 N(d_1) e^{-qT} \]
    其中 \(N'(x) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}} e^{-x^2 / 2}\)。
  • 期权多头的 Theta 通常为负值 – 随着时间流逝，期权不断损失时间价值。
  • 算例： \(S_0 = 49, K = 50, r = 0.05, \sigma = 0.20, T = 20/52\)： \[ \Theta = -4.31 \text{ / 年} = \frac{-4.31}{252} = \mathbf{-0.0171} \text{ / 交易日} \] 这意味着该期权价值每个交易日因时间流逝自然衰减约 $0.0171。

Theta (\(\Theta\))

  • Definition: Theta is the rate of change of option price \(f\) with respect to the passage of time \(t\):
    \[ \Theta = \frac{\partial f}{\partial t} \]
  • Theta of a European Call on a Stock paying Continuous Yield \(q\):
    \[ \Theta_c = -\frac{S_0 N'(d_1) \sigma e^{-qT}}{2\sqrt{T}} - r K e^{-rT} N(d_2) + q S_0 N(d_1) e^{-qT} \]
    where \(N'(x) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}} e^{-x^2 / 2}\).
  • Theta is usually negative – as time passes, the option loses time value (time decay).
  • Example: \(S_0 = 49, K = 50, r = 0.05, \sigma = 0.20, T = 20/52\): \[ \Theta = -4.31 \text{ p.a.} = \frac{-4.31}{252} = \mathbf{-0.0171} \text{ per trading day} \] The option value decays by approximately $0.0171 per trading day.

Gamma (\(\Gamma\)) 曲率

  • 定义： Gamma 是 Delta (\(\Delta\)) 相对于标的资产价格 \(S\) 的变化率（期权价格对标的资产价格的二阶偏导数）：
    \[ \Gamma = \frac{\partial \Delta}{\partial S} = \frac{\partial^2 f}{\partial S^2} \]

    \[ \Gamma = \frac{N'(d_1)}{S_0 \sigma \sqrt{T}} \quad \text{若含连续红利率 } q: \quad \Gamma = \frac{N'(d_1) e^{-qT}}{S_0 \sigma \sqrt{T}} \]

Gamma (\(\Gamma\))

  • Definition: Gamma is the rate of change of delta \(\Delta\) with respect to the price \(S\) of the underlying asset (second derivative of option price):
    \[ \Gamma = \frac{\partial \Delta}{\partial S} = \frac{\partial^2 f}{\partial S^2} \]
  • Formula for European Call and Put (identical for both!):
    \[ \Gamma = \frac{N'(d_1)}{S_0 \sigma \sqrt{T}} \quad \text{or with dividend } q: \quad \Gamma = \frac{N'(d_1) e^{-qT}}{S_0 \sigma \sqrt{T}} \]
  • If \(S\) increases by \(\Delta S\), delta changes by approximately \(\Gamma \times \Delta S\).
  • Example: \(S_0 = 49, K = 50, r = 0.05, \sigma = 0.20, T = 20/52 \implies \Gamma = 0.066, \Delta = 0.5216\).
    If stock price rises from $49 to $50.50 (\(\Delta S = \$1.50\)): \[ \Delta_{\text{new}} \approx 0.5216 + (0.066 \times 1.50) = 0.5216 + 0.099 = \mathbf{0.6206} \]

  • 对冲者必须高度关注持有平值期权的组合，其 Gamma 风险极为集中。

Vega (\(\mathcal{V}\))

  • Definition: Vega is the rate of change of option price \(f\) with respect to the volatility \(\sigma\) of the underlying asset:

  • Interpretation: If volatility \(\sigma\) increases by 1% (0.01), the option price increases by approximately \(0.01 \times \mathcal{V}\).
  • Vega is highest for at-the-money options and drops off for deep ITM/OTM options.

风险对冲的权衡考量：曲率、波动率与再平衡频率

Delta 中性 (\(\Delta = 0\))
一阶线性保护。 切线拟合。防范微小的价格渐进变动。利用高流动性现货股票或期货易于执行。
Gamma 中性 (\(\Gamma = 0\))
二阶曲率保护。 二次抛物线拟合。防范再平衡间隔期间的价格大幅跳变。消除 \(\Delta\) 的快速漂移。
Vega 中性 (\(\mathcal{V} = 0\))
波动率冲击保护。 防范市场突发体制转变与隐含波动率激增（例如金融危机或黑天鹅事件）。

实务中连续对冲与离散对冲的权衡：

  • BSM 理想状态： 零摩擦的连续再平衡可实现完全无风险对冲（\(\Delta \Pi = 0\)）。
  • 市场现实： 真实交易中，高频连续再平衡会产生巨大的交易手续费与买卖价差摩擦损耗。
  • 离散跟踪误差： 在离散时间间隔 \(\Delta t\) 内，未对冲的跟踪误差满足：\(\Delta \Pi \approx \frac{1}{2} \Gamma (\Delta S)^2 + \Theta \Delta t + \mathcal{V} \Delta \sigma\)。

Risk Management Trade-Offs: Curvature, Volatility & Rebalancing

Delta Neutrality (\(\Delta = 0\))
First-order protection. Linear tangent fit. Protects against small, incremental price moves. Easily executed with liquid underlying shares or futures.
Gamma Neutrality (\(\Gamma = 0\))
Second-order curvature. Quadratic parabolic fit. Protects against large price jumps between rebalancing intervals. Eliminates rapid drift in \(\Delta\).
Vega Neutrality (\(\mathcal{V} = 0\))
Volatility shock protection. Protects against sudden market regime shifts and implied volatility spikes (e.g. market crashes, COVID crisis).

Continuous vs Discrete Hedging in Practice:

  • The BSM Ideal: Continuous rebalancing with zero friction produces an exact riskless hedge (\(\Delta \Pi = 0\)).
  • Market Reality: In real markets, continuous rebalancing incurs prohibitive transaction costs and bid-ask spread friction.
  • Discrete Tracking Error: An unadjusted delta hedge over interval \(\Delta t\) incurs tracking variance: \(\Delta \Pi \approx \frac{1}{2} \Gamma (\Delta S)^2 + \Theta \Delta t + \mathcal{V} \Delta \sigma\).
  • Institutional Compromise: Maintain Delta neutrality daily using low-cost spot or futures contracts, and neutralize Gamma/Vega periodically when liquid exchange-traded options are economically accessible.

Dynamic Delta Hedging

  • Fundamental Principle of BSM: A portfolio consisting of \(\Delta\) units of the underlying asset and a short derivative position is locally risk-free:

动态对冲与静态对冲的对比分析

  • 动态对冲期权的总成本现值约等于其 Black-Scholes 理论价值（$240,000）。
  • 动态对冲组合构建了期权的合成多头头寸，从而精确中和了期权卖方的空头敞口。
  • 再平衡频率的权衡： 提高再平衡频率可以降低跟踪误差及对冲成本的波动，但会显著增加累积的交易手续费。
  • 利用远期合约进行静态对冲：
    • 由于远期合约的 Delta 恒等于 1（\(\Delta = 1\)），由 1 股标的股票多头与 1 份远期合约空头构成的组合完全不需要任何再平衡！
    • 远期合约可以实现“对冲后即可高枕无忧”（静态对冲）。而期权因具有内在非线性曲率，本质上依赖动态 Delta 对冲。

Dynamic Hedge vs Static Hedge

  • The present value of the total cost of hedging the option dynamically is approximately equal to its Black-Scholes theoretical value ($240,000).
  • The dynamic hedge portfolio represents a synthetic long position in the option, neutralizing the short position of the writer.
  • Rebalancing Frequency: More frequent rebalancing reduces the tracking error / variation in the cost of hedging, but increases cumulative transaction costs.
  • Static Hedge with Forward Contracts:
    • Since the delta of a forward contract is 1 (\(\Delta = 1\)), a portfolio consisting of Long 1 share of stock and Short 1 forward contract requires no rebalancing!
    • With forward contracts we can “hedge and forget” (static hedge). Options inherently require dynamic delta hedging.
