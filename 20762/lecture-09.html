支付连续复利红利收益率资产的欧式期权

考虑以当前价值为 \(S_0\)、支付连续复利红利率为 \(q\) 的资产为标的，执行价格为 \(K\)、到期期限为 \(T\) 的欧式看涨与看跌期权。

  • 按年率 \(q\) 支付连续红利会导致该资产的预期资本收益率比不支付红利的情况减少 \(q\)。
  • 在以下两种情况下，时刻 \(T\) 资产价格的概率分布完全一致：
    • 资产当前价格为 \(S_0\)，并在存续期内支付连续红利率 \(q\)
    • 资产当前价格设定为 \(S_0 e^{-qT}\)，且不提供任何红利收益
  • 我们可以通过将标的资产价格下调至 \(S_0 e^{-qT}\)，随后假设其不支付红利，以此直接对欧式期权进行估值。
  • 基本转换法则： 在所有 Black-Scholes 定价公式和买卖权平价关系中，直接将 \(S_0\) 替换为 \(S_0 e^{-qT}\)。

European Options on Assets paying Continuously Compounded Dividend Yield

We consider a European call and European put with strike \(K\) and maturity \(T\) on an asset with value \(S_0\) that pays a continuously compounded dividend yield of \(q\).

  • The payment of a dividend yield at rate \(q\) causes the expected return from the asset to be less than it would otherwise be, by the amount \(q\).
  • The same probability distribution for the asset price at time \(T\) is obtained in each of the following cases:
    • The asset price starts at price \(S_0\) and pays a dividend yield \(q\)
    • The asset price starts at price \(S_0 e^{-qT}\) and provides no dividend income
  • We can value European options by reducing the stock price to \(S_0 e^{-qT}\) and then assuming there is no dividend.
  • Fundamental Rule: In all Black-Scholes formulas and parity relations, replace \(S_0\) with \(S_0 e^{-qT}\).

价格上下限与买卖权平价定理

看涨期权价格上下限

理论下限：
\(c \ge \max(S_0 e^{-qT} - K e^{-rT}, 0)\)

理论上限：
\(c \le S_0 e^{-qT}\)

看跌期权价格上下限

理论下限：
\(p \ge \max(K e^{-rT} - S_0 e^{-qT}, 0)\)

理论上限：
\(p \le K e^{-rT}\)

连续红利收益率下的买卖权平价定理

\[ c + K e^{-rT} = p + S_0 e^{-qT} \]

回顾： 对于支付现值为 \(D\) 的离散红利股票，平价关系式为 \(c + K e^{-rT} = p + S_0 - D\)。在此处，连续红利率将标的现货价格折现了 \(e^{-qT}\)。

Bounds and Put-Call Parity

Call Bounds

Lower Bound:
\(c \ge \max(S_0 e^{-qT} - K e^{-rT}, 0)\)

Upper Bound:
\(c \le S_0 e^{-qT}\)

Put Bounds

Lower Bound:
\(p \ge \max(K e^{-rT} - S_0 e^{-qT}, 0)\)

Upper Bound:
\(p \le K e^{-rT}\)

Put-Call Parity with Continuous Dividend Yield

\[ c + K e^{-rT} = p + S_0 e^{-qT} \]

Recall: For a stock with discrete dividends of present value \(D\), the relationship was \(c + K e^{-rT} = p + S_0 - D\). Here, continuous dividend yield discounts the spot asset by \(e^{-qT}\).

二叉树模型：连续红利收益率

  • 令 \(p\) 为风险中性世界中的上涨概率。
  • 在“风险中性”世界中：
    • 所有资产的总预期收益率均为无风险利率 \(r\)。
    • 支付连续红利率 \(q\) 的股票，其预期价格增长率（资本利得）为 \(r - q\)。因此：
\[ S_0 = e^{-(r-q)\Delta t} E[S_{\Delta t}] = e^{-(r-q)\Delta t} [p S_u + (1-p) S_d] \quad (1) \] \[ f_0 = e^{-r\Delta t} E[f_{\Delta t}] = e^{-r\Delta t} [p f_u + (1-p) f_d] \quad (2) \]
  • 由式 (1)，两端除以 \(S_0\) 可得 \(e^{(r-q)\Delta t} = p u + (1-p) d\)。求解 \(p\) 得：
    \[ p = \frac{e^{(r-q)\Delta t} - d}{u - d} \]
  • 由式 (2)，每个树节点处的期权价值均按无风险利率 \(r\) 折现： \[ f_0 = e^{-r\Delta t} [p f_u + (1-p) f_d] \]

Binomial Model: Continuous Dividend Yield

  • Let \(p\) be the “risk-neutral” probability of an up movement.
  • In a “risk-neutral” world:
    • The expected return on every asset is the risk-free rate \(r\).
    • The expected capital return on a stock paying a continuous yield \(q\) is \(r - q\). Thus:
\[ S_0 = e^{-(r-q)\Delta t} E[S_{\Delta t}] = e^{-(r-q)\Delta t} [p S_u + (1-p) S_d] \quad (1) \] \[ f_0 = e^{-r\Delta t} E[f_{\Delta t}] = e^{-r\Delta t} [p f_u + (1-p) f_d] \quad (2) \]
  • From (1), dividing by \(S_0\) gives \(e^{(r-q)\Delta t} = p u + (1-p) d\). Solving for \(p\):
    \[ p = \frac{e^{(r-q)\Delta t} - d}{u - d} \]
  • From (2), the option value at each node is discounted at the risk-free rate \(r\): \[ f_0 = e^{-r\Delta t} [p f_u + (1-p) f_d] \]

Black-Scholes 定价公式（默顿扩展 / Merton Extension）

  • 欧式看涨期权价格为：
    \[ c = S_0 e^{-qT} N(d_1) - K e^{-rT} N(d_2) \]
  • 欧式看跌期权价格为：
    \[ p = K e^{-rT} N(-d_2) - S_0 e^{-qT} N(-d_1) \]

其中：

\[ d_1 = \frac{\ln(S_0 / K) + (r - q + \frac{1}{2}\sigma^2) T}{\sigma \sqrt{T}} \] \[ d_2 = \frac{\ln(S_0 / K) + (r - q - \frac{1}{2}\sigma^2) T}{\sigma \sqrt{T}} = d_1 - \sigma \sqrt{T} \]

股指期权 – 定价原理

  • 股票指数可视为提供连续复利红利率 \(q\) 的金融资产（平均反映其所有成分股的红利收益）。
  • 二叉树模型：
    \[ f_0 = e^{-r\Delta t} [p f_u + (1-p) f_d], \quad \text{其中 } p = \frac{e^{(r-q)\Delta t} - d}{u - d} \]
    其中 \(\Delta t\) 为每个时间步长的长度。
  • Black-Scholes 公式：
    \[ c = S_0 e^{-qT} N(d_1) - K e^{-rT} N(d_2), \quad p = K e^{-rT} N(-d_2) - S_0 e^{-qT} N(-d_1) \]
    其中： \[ d_1 = \frac{\ln(S_0 / K) + (r - q + \frac{1}{2}\sigma^2) T}{\sigma \sqrt{T}}, \quad d_2 = d_1 - \sigma \sqrt{T} \]

Index Options – Pricing

  • An index is an asset that provides a continuously compounded dividend yield \(q\) (on average across its component stocks).
  • Binomial Model:
    \[ f_0 = e^{-r\Delta t} [p f_u + (1-p) f_d], \quad \text{with } p = \frac{e^{(r-q)\Delta t} - d}{u - d} \]
    where \(\Delta t\) is the length of each time step.
  • Black-Scholes Formula:
    \[ c = S_0 e^{-qT} N(d_1) - K e^{-rT} N(d_2), \quad p = K e^{-rT} N(-d_2) - S_0 e^{-qT} N(-d_1) \]
    with: \[ d_1 = \frac{\ln(S_0 / K) + (r - q + \frac{1}{2}\sigma^2) T}{\sigma \sqrt{T}}, \quad d_2 = d_1 - \sigma \sqrt{T} \]

股指期权 – 基于远期价格定价

  • 期权可通过标的指数期限为 \(T\)（到期时间与期权完全相同）的远期价格 \(F_0\) 进行定价：
    \[ F_0 = S_0 e^{(r-q)T} \implies F_0 e^{-rT} = S_0 e^{-qT} \]
  • 以远期价格表示的 Black-Scholes 公式：
    \[ c = F_0 e^{-rT} N(d_1) - K e^{-rT} N(d_2) = e^{-rT} [F_0 N(d_1) - K N(d_2)] \] \[ p = K e^{-rT} N(-d_2) - F_0 e^{-rT} N(-d_1) = e^{-rT} [K N(-d_2) - F_0 N(-d_1)] \]
    其中： \[ d_1 = \frac{\ln(F_0 / K) + \frac{1}{2}\sigma^2 T}{\sigma \sqrt{T}}, \quad d_2 = \frac{\ln(F_0 / K) - \frac{1}{2}\sigma^2 T}{\sigma \sqrt{T}} = d_1 - \sigma \sqrt{T} \]
  • 核心优势： 指数的红利收益率 \(q\) 完全不出现在这些方程中，因此无需对红利率进行任何估算！

Index Options – Pricing using Forward Price

  • Options can be priced by using the forward price \(F_0\) of the index for a contract with maturity \(T\) (where \(T =\) option's maturity):
    \[ F_0 = S_0 e^{(r-q)T} \implies F_0 e^{-rT} = S_0 e^{-qT} \]
  • Black-Scholes Formula in terms of Forward Price:
    \[ c = F_0 e^{-rT} N(d_1) - K e^{-rT} N(d_2) = e^{-rT} [F_0 N(d_1) - K N(d_2)] \] \[ p = K e^{-rT} N(-d_2) - F_0 e^{-rT} N(-d_1) = e^{-rT} [K N(-d_2) - F_0 N(-d_1)] \]
    with: \[ d_1 = \frac{\ln(F_0 / K) + \frac{1}{2}\sigma^2 T}{\sigma \sqrt{T}}, \quad d_2 = \frac{\ln(F_0 / K) - \frac{1}{2}\sigma^2 T}{\sigma \sqrt{T}} = d_1 - \sigma \sqrt{T} \]
  • Key Advantage: The dividend yield \(q\) on the index does not appear in these equations, thus there is no need for it to be estimated!

利用股指期权构建投资组合保险

投资组合保险策略的核心在于构建保护性看跌期权（Protective Put） – 建立股票投资组合多头的同时，买入股指看跌期权作为下行保险。

  • 每张股指期权合约覆盖的名义价值为 \(\$100 \times S_0\)，其中 \(S_0\) 为当前指数点位。

Index Options for Portfolio Insurance

A portfolio insurance strategy is to construct a protective put – a long position in the equity portfolio and a long position in put index options.

  • One index option contract covers \(\$100 \times S_0\), where \(S_0\) is the current index level.
  • A portfolio of value \(P\) with beta \(\beta\) is insured by buying \(N\) put options:
    \[ N = \beta \frac{P}{100 \times S_0} \]
  • The strike price \(K\) of the put options is chosen to establish the desired minimum portfolio value (the floor).
  • The cost of the insurance is the option premium required to purchase the puts.
  • Advantages over static hedging / forward sales:
    • A long option represents a right, not an obligation – downside protection while retaining full upside potential!
    • Flexibility to choose any strike price \(K\) to customize the deductible level and premium cost.

期货期权 – 收益结构

  • 期货看涨期权： 赋予持有者在约定到期日或之前以执行价格 \(K\) 建立期货合约多头的权利。
    • 行权时，持有者获得一份标的期货合约的多头头寸，并
    • 获得一笔等于最新期货结算价 \(F^*\) 超过执行价格部分额度的现金：\(\max(F^* - K, 0)\)。
  • 期货看跌期权： 赋予持有者在约定到期日或之前以执行价格 \(K\) 建立期货合约空头的权利。
    • 行权时，持有者获得一份标的期货合约的空头头寸，并
    • 获得一笔等于执行价格超过最新期货结算价部分额度的现金：\(\max(K - F^*, 0)\)。
  • 若行权后获得的期货头寸立即平仓：
    \[ \text{看涨期权收益} = \max(F_T - K, 0), \quad \text{看跌期权收益} = \max(K - F_T, 0) \]
    其中 \(F_T\) 为行权时刻的期货市场价格。
  • 期权的到期日通常设定在标的期货合约交割日当天或交割日前数个交易日。

Futures Options – Payoffs

  • Call Option on Futures: Right to enter into a long futures contract by a certain date at strike \(K\).
    • When exercised, the holder acquires a long position in the futures contract, plus
    • A cash amount equal to the excess of the most recent futures settlement price \(F^*\) over strike: \(\max(F^* - K, 0)\).
  • Put Option on Futures: Right to enter into a short futures contract by a certain date at strike \(K\).
    • When exercised, the holder acquires a short position in the futures contract, plus
    • A cash amount equal to the excess of strike over the futures settlement price: \(\max(K - F^*, 0)\).
  • If the resulting futures position is closed out immediately:
    \[ \text{Call Payoff} = \max(F_T - K, 0), \quad \text{Put Payoff} = \max(K - F_T, 0) \]
    where \(F_T\) is the futures price at the time of exercise.
  • The option's maturity is usually on, or a few days before, the delivery date of the underlying futures contract.

期货期权 – 定价（Black 模型）

  • 在风险中性世界中，期货合约的预期漂移率为零：
    • 进入期货合约的初始成本为零；零成本投资的期望利润必须为零。
    • 直接使用连续红利收益率定价公式，代入 \(S_0 = F_0\) 以及 \(q = r\)。
  • 买卖权平价定理：
    \[ c + K e^{-rT} = p + F_0 e^{-rT} \]
  • 期货二叉树模型： \[ f_0 = e^{-r\Delta t} [p f_u + (1-p) f_d], \quad \text{其中 } p = \frac{e^{(r-r)\Delta t} - d}{u - d} = \frac{1 - d}{u - d} \]
  • Black (1976) 期货期权定价模型：
    \[ c = e^{-rT} [F_0 N(d_1) - K N(d_2)], \quad p = e^{-rT} [K N(-d_2) - F_0 N(-d_1)] \]
    其中： \[ d_1 = \frac{\ln(F_0 / K) + \frac{1}{2}\sigma^2 T}{\sigma \sqrt{T}}, \quad d_2 = d_1 - \sigma \sqrt{T} \]

期货期权与现货期权的比较分析

  • 相比现货期权，期货期权的潜在竞争优势：
    • 期货合约通常比标的物理现货资产具有更高的流动性，交易更为便捷（例如国债现货存在最便宜可交割券与应计利息的复杂性）。
    • 行权无需立即进行实物现货交割；进入期货合约推迟了现金流出，保留了货币时间价值。
    • 标的物理资产可能难以直接交易或做空（例如大宗农产品、缺乏现货流动性的指数）。
    • 期货期权通常享有更低的交易手续费、更窄的买卖价差与交易所统一清算担保。
  • 美式期权估值对比分析：
    • 正常市场（期货升水 / Contango，\(F_0 > S_0\)）： 期货美式看涨期权价值高于现货美式看涨期权；期货美式看跌期权价值低于现货美式看跌期权。
    • 反向市场（期货贴水 / Backwardation，\(F_0 < S_0\)）： 结论完全相反 – 期货看跌期权价值更高，期货看涨期权价值更低。

Futures Options and Spot Options

  • Potential advantages of futures options over spot options:
    • Futures contracts are frequently more liquid and easier to trade than the underlying physical asset (e.g. government bonds with cheapest-to-deliver and accrued interest complications).
    • Exercising does not require immediate physical delivery; entering a futures contract postpones cash outlay, preserving the time value of money.
    • The underlying asset may not be readily available for trading or shorting (e.g. agricultural commodities, illiquid spot indexes).
    • Futures options often entail lower transaction costs, tighter bid-ask spreads, and uniform clearing.
  • Valuation Comparison for American Options:
    • Normal Market (Contango, \(F_0 > S_0\)): An American call on futures is worth more than an American call on spot. An American put on futures is worth less than an American put on spot.
    • Inverted Market (Backwardation, \(F_0 < S_0\)): The reverse is true – futures put is worth more, futures call is worth less.
