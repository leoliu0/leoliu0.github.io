利率互换 (Interest Rate Swaps)

  • 利率互换的核心结构特征：
    • 期初不交换本金：名义本金 (Notional principal) 仅作为计算利息收付金额的基准尺度。
    • 利息差额净额结算：在每个结算日，交易双方仅结算并支付浮动利息与固定利息的净差额。
    • 期初设定、期末结算 (Reset in advance, paid in arrears)：浮动利率在每个计息期初锁定，产生的实际现金流在计息期末进行支付。
  • 核心经济功能：
    • 将负债 (Liability) 由固定利率转换为浮动利率，或由浮动利率转换为固定利率。
    • 将投资资产 (Asset) 由固定利率转换为浮动利率，反之亦然。
  • 历史背景：全球首笔正式互换协议于 1981 年在 IBM 与世界银行 (World Bank) 之间签署生效。

Interest Rate Swaps

  • Key structural features of interest rate swaps:
    • No initial exchange of principal: Notional principal serves only as the scale for calculating interest payments.
    • Netting of interest payments: At each settlement date, only the net interest difference changes hands between counterparties.
    • Reset in advance, paid in arrears: Floating interest rates are fixed at the beginning of each period, with the resulting cash flow paid at the end of the period.
  • Core economic functions:
    • Convert a liability from fixed rate to floating rate, or from floating rate to fixed rate.
    • Convert an investment (asset) from fixed rate to floating rate, or vice versa.
  • Historical note: The first formal swap was executed in 1981 between IBM and the World Bank.

案例 – 英特尔与微软转换负债结构

英特尔公司 (Intel)

在债券市场按 5.20% 固定利率发行债券借入 $100M（1亿美元）。

微软公司 (Microsoft)

在银行间市场按 LIBOR + 0.10% 浮动利率借入 $100M（1亿美元）。

英特尔 (Intel)
← 支付 LIBOR ←
→ 支付 5.00% →
微软 (MS)
英特尔净负债头寸：
向市场付 5.2% • 从微软收 5% • 向微软付 LIBOR：

利率互换现金流时间线与风险转换

  • 期初锁定、期末结算 (Fixing in Advance, Payment in Arrears)：基准浮动利率（例如 3 个月期 LIBOR / BBSW）在季度初 (\( t_i \)) 确定，但净现金流结算在季度末 (\( t_{i+1} \)) 执行。
  • 差额净额结算 (Netting Settlement)：名义本金不发生实际交换。交易双方仅结算并支付净利息差额： \[ \text{净现金流} = (r_{\text{fixed}} - r_{\text{floating}}) \times \text{名义本金} \times \Delta t \]
  • 合成固定利率负债 (Synthetic Fixed Liability)：持有与基准利率挂钩浮动银行债务的企业可彻底消除利率上升风险： \[ \text{实际综合成本} = -(\text{浮动利率} + \text{利差}) + \text{浮动利率} - \text{固定利率} = -(\text{固定利率} + \text{利差}) \]

利率走势与择时考量：

银行互换报价中的固定利率走势独立于央行当前的短期基准利率。固定互换利率反映的是长达数年的未来远期利率预期与期限溢价。等待央行短期基准利率见底，并不能保证锁定更低的长期固定互换利率。

IRS Cash Flow Timeline & Risk Transformation

Core Operational Mechanics Explained in Video:

  • Fixing in Advance, Payment in Arrears: The reference floating rate (e.g. 3-month LIBOR / BBSW) is fixed at the start of the quarter (\( t_i \)), but the net cash flow is settled only at the end of the quarter (\( t_{i+1} \)).
  • Netting Settlement: Principal is never exchanged. Counterparties settle solely the net interest differential: \[ \text{Cash Flow} = (r_{\text{fixed}} - r_{\text{floating}}) \times \text{Notional} \times \Delta t \]
  • Synthetic Fixed Liability: A business with variable bank debt linked to base rate eliminates rising rate risk: \[ \text{Effective Cost} = -(\text{Floating} + \text{Spread}) + \text{Floating} - \text{Fixed} = -(\text{Fixed} + \text{Spread}) \]

Market Timing Warning (Video Takeaway):

Bank swap quotes for fixed rates move independently of current central bank base rates. Fixed swap rates reflect multi-year forward expectations and term premia. Waiting for base rates to hit rock bottom does not guarantee a lower fixed swap rate!

互换市场中的金融中介机构 (FIs)

  • 金融机构作为互换市场的做市商 (Market Makers)：
    • 市场上两家拥有完全对称相反需求的企业同时找到银行的概率微乎其微。
    • 绝大多数互换是由金融机构先与单方交易对手达成，此时并无立即可对冲的反向头寸——金融机构对该互换风险进行“仓储” (warehouses the swap)。
    • 金融机构通过收取买卖买卖价差或佣金赚取利润（通常为 3–4 个基点，bps）。
  • 金融机构对未平仓风险敞口的动态对冲：
    • 市场风险（利率风险）：利用利率期货（如欧洲美元期货或银行承兑汇票期货）、政府国债以及反向互换交易实施动态对冲。
    • 信用（违约）风险：通过要求交易对手缴纳抵押品（信用支持协议 CSA）、签署净额结算协议，以及在信用违约互换 (CDS) 市场买入保护来管理。

Financial Institutions (FIs) in the Swap Market

  • FIs act as Market Makers for swaps:
    • It is very unlikely that two counterparties with exact offsetting needs will approach the bank simultaneously.
    • Most swaps are entered into by the FI with one counterparty without an immediately offsetting position – the FI “warehouses” the swap.
    • The FI charges a bid-ask spread or commission (typically 3–4 basis points).
  • The FI must actively hedge its open risk:
    • Market Risk (Interest Rate Risk): Hedged dynamically using interest rate futures (e.g. Eurodollar or bank bill futures), government bonds, and offsetting swap transactions.
    • Credit (Default) Risk: Managed by demanding collateral (credit support annex – CSA), netting agreements, and hedging in credit default swap (CDS) markets.

比较优势理论 (The Comparative Advantage Argument)

市场向两家公司提供的直接借款利率报价如下：

借款公司 固定利率市场 浮动利率市场
AAACorp（AAA级） 4.00% 6个月 LIBOR − 0.10%
BBBCorp（BBB级） 5.20% 6个月 LIBOR + 0.60%
利率差额 (Spread) 1.20% 0.70%
  • 剖析借款利差的显著差异：
    • AAACorp 在固定利率市场借款利率低 1.20%，但在浮动利率市场仅低 0.70%。
    • AAACorp 在固定利率市场具有比较优势。
    • BBBCorp 在浮动利率市场具有比较优势。
  • 若 AAACorp 期望承担浮动债务，而 BBBCorp 期望承担固定债务，互换使双方可在各自具有比较优势的市场借款，再通过互换获得期望的负债结构！

The Comparative Advantage Argument

The following borrowing rates are offered to two corporations:

Company Fixed Rate Market Floating Rate Market
AAACorp 4.00% 6-month LIBOR − 0.10%
BBBCorp 5.20% 6-month LIBOR + 0.60%
Difference (Spread) 1.20% 0.70%
  • Notice the spread differential:
    • AAACorp pays 1.20% less in the fixed market, but only 0.70% less in the floating market.
    • AAACorp has a comparative advantage in the FIXED market.
    • BBBCorp has a comparative advantage in the FLOATING market.
  • If AAACorp wants floating debt and BBBCorp wants fixed debt, a swap allows each party to borrow where it has a comparative advantage and swap into its desired debt profile!

      借款总成本 = \( \text{LIBOR} + 4.60\% \)。

More about Interest Rate Swaps

  • Alternative Swap Designs:
    • Instead of passing through floating and adjusting fixed, the parties can exchange plain LIBOR and adjust the fixed rate legs accordingly (e.g. AAA pays LIBOR, gets 4.33%; BBB pays 4.37%, gets LIBOR → FI earns 4.37% − 4.33% = 0.04%).

    • Rollover Risk: The floating rates (LIBOR − 0.10% and LIBOR + 0.60%) are 6-month borrowing facilities. The credit spread is reviewed every 6 months!

利率互换估值原理 (Valuation of IRS)

利率互换在理论上可分解为两份债券的价值差额进行估值：

  • 固定利率债券 (\( B_{\text{fix}} \))：按常规债券定价法，利用无风险零息利率曲线折现所有未来的固定票息及本金现金流。
  • 浮动利率债券 (\( B_{\text{fl}} \))：利用其最根本的定价特性——浮动利率债券在每次付息日刚过之后，其理论价值恰好等于面值 (Par)： \[ B_{\text{fl}} = (L + k^*) e^{-r t_1} \] 其中 \( L \) 为名义本金，\( k^* \) 为在上一重置日已确定的下一次浮动利息支付额，\( t_1 \) 为距离下一次付息的时间。
  • 互换合约净价值：
    \[ V_{\text{swap}} = B_{\text{fl}} - B_{\text{fix}} \quad \text{（针对收取浮动利率、支付固定利率方）} \] \[ V_{\text{swap}} = B_{\text{fix}} - B_{\text{fl}} \quad \text{（针对收取固定利率、支付浮动利率方）} \]
  • 备选估值法：互换亦可等价分解为一系列远期利率协议 (FRAs) 的投资组合进行分别定价。

Valuation of an Interest Rate Swap

An interest rate swap can be valued as the difference between two bonds:

  • Fixed-rate bond (\( B_{\text{fix}} \)): Valued in the usual way by discounting all scheduled fixed coupon and principal cash flows using the zero curve.
  • Floating-rate bond (\( B_{\text{fl}} \)): Valued by using the fundamental property that a floating-rate bond is worth exactly par immediately after every payment date: \[ B_{\text{fl}} = (L + k^*) e^{-r t_1} \] where \( L \) is notional principal, \( k^* \) is the known upcoming floating payment set at the last reset date, and \( t_1 \) is time until the next payment.
  • Net Swap Value:
    \[ V_{\text{swap}} = B_{\text{fl}} - B_{\text{fix}} \quad \text{(for party receiving floating, paying fixed)} \] \[ V_{\text{swap}} = B_{\text{fix}} - B_{\text{fl}} \quad \text{(for party receiving fixed, paying floating)} \]
  • Alternative approach: Valued as a portfolio of Forward Rate Agreements (FRAs).

货币互换估值方法 (Valuation of Currency Swaps)

与利率互换类似，货币互换可以通过以下两种等价方法估值：

  • 分解为两份不同货币债券价值的差额。
  • 分解为一系列远期外汇合约 (FX Forwards) 的投资组合。

基于债券组合价格的估值法：

  • 需要当前即期汇率 \( S_0 \)（定义为每单位外币折合的本币数额，即直接标价法）。
  • 令 \( B_D \) 为本币债券在本币计价下的价值。
  • 令 \( B_F \) 为外币债券在外币计价下的价值。
  • 对于收取本币 \( D \)、支付外币 \( F \) 的金融机构：
    \[ V_{\text{swap}} = B_D - S_0 B_F \]
  • 对于收取外币 \( F \)、支付本币 \( D \) 的金融机构：
    \[ V_{\text{swap}} = S_0 B_F - B_D \]

Valuation of a Currency Swap

Like interest rate swaps, currency swaps can be valued as:

  • The difference between the values of two bonds.
  • A portfolio of forward foreign exchange contracts.

Valuation in Terms of Bond Prices:

  • The current spot exchange rate \( S_0 \) is required (defined as domestic currency per unit of foreign currency).
  • Let \( B_D \) be the value of the domestic currency bond (in domestic currency).
  • Let \( B_F \) be the value of the foreign currency bond (in foreign currency).
  • For a financial institution receiving domestic currency \( D \) and paying foreign currency \( F \):
    \[ V_{\text{swap}} = B_D - S_0 B_F \]
  • For a financial institution receiving foreign currency \( F \) and paying domestic currency \( D \):
    \[ V_{\text{swap}} = S_0 B_F - B_D \]

信用（违约）风险 (Credit Risk)

  • 信用风险源于交易对手违约的可能性：
    • 在合约起初时刻，互换理论价值严格为零 (\( V_0 = 0 \))。
    • 随着时间推移，市场利率与外汇汇率持续波动，导致互换价值对一方转为正值，对另一方相应转为负值。
  • 金融机构面临的非对称信用风险暴露 (Asymmetric Credit Exposure)：
    • 金融机构仅当互换对其具有正价值时，才面临真实的信用风险暴露。
    • 若互换对金融机构的价值为负，对手方绝无理由违约，因为该合约对对手方而言是一项具有正市场价值的资产（若对手方破产，其清算人亦会保留或出售该资产）。
  • 互换违约与普通贷款违约损失对比：
    • 互换违约导致的潜在损失远小于同等名义本金贷款的违约损失，因为利率互换中本金从未被实际借贷给对方。
    • 在发生违约时，货币互换造成的损失显著大于利率互换，因为货币互换涉及期末本金的全额收付交换，且两国汇率可能经历极其剧烈的累计漂移。

Credit (Default) Risk

  • Credit risk arises from the possibility of default by a counterparty:
    • At initiation, a swap is worth zero (\( V_0 = 0 \)).
    • Over time, interest rates and currency rates fluctuate, causing the swap value to become positive to one party and negative to the other.
  • Asymmetric Credit Exposure for Financial Institutions:
    • An FI is exposed to credit risk only when the swap value is positive to the FI.
    • If the swap value to the FI is negative, the counterparty will not default because the contract is an asset to them (or bankruptcy administrators will preserve/sell it).
  • Default Losses on Swaps vs Loans:
    • Potential losses from defaults on swaps are much smaller than losses on loans of the same notional principal, because principals are not lent out (in IRS).
    • At default, currency swaps generate significantly greater losses than interest rate swaps because principal amounts are exchanged and exchange rates can experience massive cumulative shifts.
