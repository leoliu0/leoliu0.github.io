期权与标的资产的头寸组合

  • 将期权与其标的资产进行组合：
    • 期权头寸可以与标的股票的多头或空头头寸相结合，从而改变投资组合的风险-收益特征。
  • 主要经典策略：
    • 备兑看涨期权 (Covered Call)：
      • 为持有标的资产的投资者提供一定的下行缓冲/保护。
      • 在平稳或温和上涨的市场环境中获取期权费权利金收益。
    • 保护性看跌期权 (Protective Put)：
      • 提供明确且有保证的短期下行保护（充当投资组合保险）。
      • 若标的资产大幅飙升，仍能完全保留无限的上方盈利潜力。

Positions in Options and Underlying Assets

  • Combining options with their underlying assets:
    • Option positions can be combined with long or short positions in the underlying stock to alter the portfolio's risk-return profile.
  • Primary Examples:
    • Covered Call:
      • Provides downside buffer / protection to investors holding the underlying asset.
      • Generates premium income in a stable or mildly bullish market.
    • Protective Put:
      • Provides guaranteed short-term downside protection (acts as portfolio insurance).
      • Preserves unlimited upside profit potential if the underlying asset rallies.

Covered Call

  • Writing a covered call:
    • Long position in the asset and short position in the call option: \( S_0 - c \)
    • Final payoff profile is isomorphic to a short put:
      From put-call parity: \( c + K e^{-rT} = p + S_0 \)

      \( S_0 - c = -p + K e^{-rT} \)

    • Short position in the stock and long position in the call option: \( -S_0 + c \)
    • Final payoff profile is isomorphic to a long put:
      \( -S_0 + c = p - K e^{-rT} \)

备兑看涨期权：策略属性

  • 适用市场环境：市场走势平稳中性或温和看涨。
  • 策略优势：
    • 在买入股票的同时卖出看涨期权，可以降低股票的净购入成本。
    • 对已持有的股票卖出看涨期权，可带来持续的期权费权利金现金流；若股价下跌，该期权费可提供一定的下行缓冲垫。
    • 持有标的股票可以完全对冲股价暴涨带来的损失风险（避免了裸卖空看涨期权的无限风险）。
  • 盈亏平衡点：\( S_0 - c = \$48 - \$1 = \mathbf{\$47} \)。
  • 策略特性：
    • 在牛市行情中，虚值备兑看涨期权 (OTM) 具有最高的盈利潜力。
    • 在平稳行情或轻度熊市行情中，实值备兑看涨期权 (ITM) 能够提供最充裕的期权费安全垫。
  • 面临问题 / 权衡取舍：上方盈利潜力被严格封顶（上限为 \( K - S_0 + c = \$3 \)），而若股价大幅暴跌，依然面临显著的下行亏损风险（最大亏损可达 \( -S_0 + c = -\$47 \)）。

Covered Call: Strategy Properties

  • Market profile: Neutral market or mildly bullish market.
  • Benefits:
    • When buying shares, shorting call options simultaneously reduces the net purchase cost.
    • Writing calls against shares you already own generates regular premium income; the income provides downside cushion if the stock declines.
    • Owning the shares provides complete protection against large losses if the stock surges (avoids naked call risk).
  • Breakeven point: \( S_0 - c = \$48 - \$1 = \mathbf{\$47} \).
  • Properties:
    • In a bullish market, OTM covered calls provide the highest profit potential.
    • In a stable/moderately bearish market, ITM covered calls provide the best premium income cushion.
  • Problem / Trade-off: Limited profit potential (capped at \( K - S_0 + c = \$3 \)) and potentially large downside loss if the stock falls severely (down to \( -S_0 + c = -\$47 \)).

保护性看跌期权：策略属性

  • 适用市场环境：对标的资产长期看涨，但对短期市场下行风险保持谨慎。
  • 策略优势：投资组合保险 (Portfolio insurance)
    • 严格锁定最大可能亏损：下行亏损被锁定为 \( S_0 + p - K = \$48 + \$3 - \$50 = \mathbf{\$1} \)。
    • 若股票价格大幅飙升，依然享有完全无限的上方盈利空间。
  • 盈亏平衡点：\( S_0 + p = \$48 + \$3 = \mathbf{\$51} \)。
  • 策略特性：
    • 虚值看跌期权 (OTM) 购买成本较低，但提供的下行保护不及实值看跌期权 (ITM)。
    • 一旦股票价格上涨突破盈亏平衡线，该策略即可实现盈利。
  • 面临问题：需要预先支付现金权利金。看跌期权费相当于保险费，在强劲牛市中会降低投资组合的整体收益率。

Protective Put: Strategy Properties

  • Market profile: Bullish underlying view, but cautious about downside market risk.
  • Benefits: Portfolio insurance
    • Provides strictly limited loss potential: downside loss is capped at \( S_0 + p - K = \$48 + \$3 - \$50 = \mathbf{\$1} \).
    • Provides completely unlimited upside profit potential if the stock surges.
  • Breakeven point: \( S_0 + p = \$48 + \$3 = \mathbf{\$51} \).
  • Properties:
    • OTM put options are cheaper to buy, but provide less downside protection than ITM put options.
    • The strategy becomes profitable once the stock price rises above the breakeven level.
  • Problem: Requires an upfront cash premium. The put premium acts as an insurance premium that reduces portfolio performance in a bull market.

备兑看涨期权 vs 保护性看跌期权：不对称风险与收益特征

核心策略差异：备兑看涨期权是一种收益增强策略 (Income-generation)，通过放弃上方收益潜力换取前期的现金期权费。而保护性看跌期权则是一种资本保全策略 (Capital preservation)，通过前期支付现金成本来彻底消除灾难性的尾部下行风险。

分析维度 备兑看涨期权 (\(S_0 - c\)) 保护性看跌期权 (\(S_0 + p\))
首要目标 获取权利金收益；在盘整市场中增强回报 防范市场崩盘风险；保全资本
期初现金流 现金净流入 (+) 来自卖出看涨期权 现金净流出 (−) 用于买入看跌期权
最大利润 封顶上限：\( K - S_0 + c \) 无限盈利：\( S_T - (S_0 + p) \)
最大亏损 实质性下行风险：\( S_0 - c \) (若股价跌至 $0) 严格锁定上限：\( S_0 + p - K \)
盈亏平衡点 下移至：\( S_0 - c \) 上移至：\( S_0 + p \)
合成头寸等价性 经济实质等同于卖出看跌期权 (Short Put)：\( -p + K e^{-rT} \) 经济实质等同于买入看涨期权 (Long Call)：\( c + K e^{-rT} \)
根据买卖权平价定理公式 \( S_0 - c = -p + K e^{-rT} \)，卖出备兑看涨期权的投资者所承担的经济风险剖面与单纯卖出看跌期权完全一致：有限的上方收益与几乎全部的下行暴跌风险。

Covered Call vs Protective Put: Asymmetric Risk & Profit Profiles

Strategic Distinction: The covered call is an income-generation strategy that surrenders upside for upfront cash yield. The protective put is a capital preservation strategy that pays cash upfront to eliminate catastrophic tail risk.

Dimension Covered Call (\(S_0 - c\)) Protective Put (\(S_0 + p\))
Primary Objective Generate yield; enhance returns in stagnant market Insure against market crashes; preserve capital
Upfront Cash Flow Cash Inflow (+) from writing the call Cash Outflow (−) to buy the put
Maximum Profit Capped at: \( K - S_0 + c \) Unlimited: \( S_T - (S_0 + p) \)
Maximum Loss Substantial downside: \( S_0 - c \) (if stock goes to $0) Strictly Capped at: \( S_0 + p - K \)
Breakeven Point Lowered to: \( S_0 - c \) Raised to: \( S_0 + p \)
Synthetic Identity Behaves like a Short Put: \( -p + K e^{-rT} \) Behaves like a Long Call: \( c + K e^{-rT} \)
Bionic Turtle Because put-call parity enforces \( S_0 - c = -p + K e^{-rT} \), an investor writing a covered call assumes the exact same economic risk profile as a short put seller: limited upside and full downside exposure.

差价策略 (Spreads)

  • 定义：差价策略是指在同一种标的资产上，同时持有两种或两种以上同类型期权（即两只或多只看涨期权，或两只或多只看跌期权）的组合头寸。
  • 主要差价策略分类：
    • 牛市差价 (Bull spreads)：
      • 利用看涨期权构建的牛市差价 (借方差价 Debit Spread)
      • 利用看跌期权构建的牛市差价 (贷方差价 Credit Spread)
    • 熊市差价 (Bear spreads)：
      • 利用看涨期权构建的熊市差价 (贷方差价 Credit Spread)
      • 利用看跌期权构建的熊市差价 (借方差价 Debit Spread)
    • 蝶式差价 (Butterfly spreads)：
      • 利用看涨期权构建的蝶式差价
      • 利用看跌期权构建的蝶式差价
    • 日历差价 (Calendar spreads)：
      • 相同行权价，不同到期日

Spreads

  • Definition: A spread is a position in two or more options of the same type (i.e., two or more calls, or two or more puts) on the same underlying asset.
  • Major Types of Spreads:
    • Bull spreads:
      • Bull spread using calls (debit spread)
      • Bull spread using puts (credit spread)

Calendar Spread

  • Core Concept: Use options with the same strike price \( K \) but different expiration dates.
  • Calendar spread using Calls:
    • Sell a short-dated call option and buy a longer-dated call option.
    • Capitalizes on the fact that the short-dated option decays faster (higher \(\theta\)) than the long-dated option.

  • Calendar spread using Puts:
    • Sell a short-dated put option and buy a longer-dated put option.
  • Reverse Calendar Spread:
    • Buy a short-dated option and sell a longer-dated option; profitable when stock makes large directional moves.

买入 vs 卖出跨式期权：波动率机制与双重盈亏平衡点

CME 权威定义：“脚踏两只船 (Straddling the fence)” — 同时押注双向。当交易者预期价格即将发生颠覆性突破但无法确定具体突破方向时（例如在财报公布前、重大监管审批揭晓或 CPI 通胀数据发布前），买入跨式期权是标准操作。

买入跨式期权 (买入看涨 + 买入看跌)
  • 总成本：\( V_0 = c + p \)（最大亏损发生在 \( S_T = K \) 时）。
  • 上方盈亏平衡点：\( S_{T, ext{upper}} = K + (c + p) \)
  • 下方盈亏平衡点：\( S_{T, ext{lower}} = K - (c + p) \)
  • 希腊字母特征：显著的正 Vega (\( \mathcal{V} > 0 \)) 与正 Gamma (\( \Gamma > 0 \))；承受负 Theta (\( \Theta < 0 \)) 时间价值侵蚀。
卖出跨式期权 (卖出看涨 + 卖出看跌)
  • 最大利润：\( c + p \)（在市场收盘恰好等于行权价 \( K \) 时获得）。
  • 盈亏平衡点：完全相同（\( K \pm (c + p) \)）。
  • 风险特征：若标的股票向任意一侧发生大幅跳跃并穿透平衡点，亏损潜力无限。
  • 希腊字母特征：正 Theta (\( \Theta > 0 \)，赚取时间价值衰减) 与负 Vega (\( \mathcal{V} < 0 \))。
CME 实务案例：对于总权利金为 105 点的 E-mini 标普指数 2420 点跨式组合，精确的盈亏平衡点分别为 \( 2420 + 105 = \mathbf{2525} \) 与 \( 2420 - 105 = \mathbf{2315} \)。

Long vs Short Straddles: Volatility Mechanics & Dual Breakevens

CME Definition: “Straddling the fence” — supporting both sides. A long straddle is executed when a trader anticipates a massive price breakout but is uncertain of the direction (e.g., ahead of earnings announcements, regulatory decisions, or CPI releases).

Long Straddle (Buy Call + Buy Put)
  • Total Cost: \( V_0 = c + p \) (maximum loss occurs at \( S_T = K \)).
  • Upper Breakeven: \( S_{T,\text{upper}} = K + (c + p) \)
  • Lower Breakeven: \( S_{T,\text{lower}} = K - (c + p) \)
  • Greeks: Highly positive Vega (\( \mathcal{V} > 0 \)) and Gamma (\( \Gamma > 0 \)); suffers from negative Theta (\( \Theta < 0 \)) time decay.
Short Straddle (Sell Call + Sell Put)
  • Max Profit: \( c + p \) (achieved if market closes exactly at strike \( K \)).
  • Breakevens: Identical (\( K \pm (c + p) \)).
  • Risk Profile: Unlimited loss potential if stock makes a dramatic move beyond either breakeven.
  • Greeks: Positive Theta (\( \Theta > 0 \), profits from time decay) and negative Vega (\( \mathcal{V} < 0 \)).
CME Example: For an E-mini 2420 straddle trading at 105 points total premium, the exact breakeven points are \( 2420 + 105 = \mathbf{2525} \) and \( 2420 - 105 = \mathbf{2315} \).

Comparison of Short Volatility Strategies

  • Straddle: Offers the maximum return, with profit peaking at the strike price ($5.75 in example), but carries unlimited loss potential on both sides.
  • Strangle: Provides a flat maximum return between strikes ($5.50 and $6.00), but also carries unlimited loss potential on both sides.
  • Butterfly: Provides a peaked return like the straddle; however, maximum profit is reduced because of the purchase of OTM wings to cap losses. The butterfly is essentially a risk-managed modification of the straddle.
  • Condor: Exhibits a flat pattern of maximum returns with strictly limited risk on both sides; makes the smallest maximum profit. It is a risk-managed modification of the strangle.
  • Capitalization & Risk:
    • The butterfly is a superb trade for under-capitalized traders who cannot afford the unlimited tail risk of naked straddles and strangles.
    • Naked straddles and strangles should only be undertaken by market professionals with adequate capital to withstand large adverse moves.
