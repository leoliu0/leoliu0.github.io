风险敞口

  • 资产多头风险敞口 (Long Asset Exposure)：
    • 已经拥有现货资产，或已签订协议将在未来生产该资产。
    • 计划在未来特定时间将其出售。
    • 面临风险： 资产价格下跌风险 → 对冲策略：建立期货空头头寸 (Short Futures)。
  • 资产空头风险敞口 (Short Asset Exposure)：
    • 在未来某一时间需要采购该资产。
    • 面临风险： 资产价格上涨风险 → 对冲策略：建立期货多头头寸 (Long Futures)。

Risk Exposure

  • Long Asset Exposure:
    • Already own the asset or have contracted to produce it.
    • Wish to sell it in the future.
    • Risk: Asset price falls → hedge by taking a Short Futures position.
  • Short Asset Exposure:
    • Need to purchase the asset in the future.
    • Risk: Asset price rises → hedge by taking a Long Futures position.

套期保值中的基差

  • 在套期保值环境下，基差 (Basis) 定义为：
    \[ \text{基差} = b_t = S_t - F_t \]
  • 其中 \(S_t\) 为被对冲资产的现货即期价格，\(F_t\) 为所使用的期货合约价格。
  • 若被对冲的资产与期货合约的标的资产完全一致，则在合约到期时刻 \(T\)： \[ b_T = S_T - F_T = 0 \]
  • 在到期日之前，基差可正可负，并随时间动态波动。

The Basis in a Hedging Situation

  • In a hedging situation, the basis is defined as:
    \[ \text{Basis} = b_t = S_t - F_t \]
  • where \(S_t\) is the spot price of the asset being hedged, and \(F_t\) is the futures price of the contract used.
  • If the asset being hedged and the asset underlying the futures contract are identical, then at contract maturity \(T\): \[ b_T = S_T - F_T = 0 \]
  • Prior to maturity, the basis can be positive or negative, and fluctuates over time.

空头套期保值

  • 空头套期保值 (Short Hedge) 是指在期货市场上建立期货空头（卖出）头寸。
  • 适用于套期保值者已经拥有某种资产、并预计在未来某个日期将其出售的情形。
  • 或者适用于当前尚未拥有该资产、但在未来将生产并销售该资产的企业。
  • 对冲机制：
    • 若现货价格下跌，现货出售端的亏损可被期货空头头寸的盈利所对冲弥补。
    • 若现货价格上涨，现货出售端的收益将被期货空头头寸的亏损所相互抵消。

Short Hedge

  • A short hedge involves taking a short position in a futures contract.
  • It is appropriate when the hedger already owns an asset and expects to sell it at some future date.
  • Alternatively, it is appropriate when an asset is not currently owned but will be produced and sold in the future.
  • Mechanism:
    • If the spot price falls, the loss on the spot sale is offset by the gain on the short futures position.
    • If the spot price rises, the gain on the spot sale is offset by the loss on the short futures position.

案例分析 – 空头套期保值 (玉米生产商)

  • 3 月份： 某农场主预计将在 9 月份收获并销售 50,000 蒲式耳玉米。
  • 当前 CBOT 市场上 9 月到期的玉米期货价格为 $5.20 / 蒲式耳。
  • 每张合约规格 = 5,000 蒲式耳 → 农场主做空卖出 10 张 9 月玉米期货合约。
  • 9 月份最终结果：
    • 假定现货价格跌至 $4.80。到期期货价格收敛至 $4.80。
    • 在当地现货市场出售玉米现货的收入：\( 50,000 \times \$4.80 = \$240,000 \)。
    • 期货空头头寸获得的盈利：\( 50,000 \times (\$5.20 - \$4.80) = \$20,000 \)。
    • 销售总净收益： \( \$240,000 + \$20,000 = \mathbf{\$260,000} \)（锁定 \( \$5.20/\text{蒲式耳} \) 的销售价格！）。

Example – Short Hedge (Corn Producer)

  • March: A farmer expects to harvest and sell 50,000 bushels of corn in September.
  • September corn futures price is currently $5.20 / bushel on CBOT.
  • Contract size = 5,000 bushels → Farmer shorts 10 September futures contracts.
  • September Outcome:
    • Suppose spot price drops to $4.80. Futures price converges to $4.80.
    • Revenue from selling crop in local market: \( 50,000 \times \$4.80 = \$240,000 \).
    • Gain on short futures: \( 50,000 \times (\$5.20 - \$4.80) = \$20,000 \).
    • Total Proceeds: \( \$240,000 + \$20,000 = \mathbf{\$260,000} \) (\( \$5.20/\text{bu} \) locked in!).

空头套期保值与基差

  • 假定某空头对冲策略在时刻 \(t_1\) 建立，并在时刻 \(t_2\) 平仓结束：
    • \(F_1\)：\(t_1\) 时刻建立对冲时的期货价格
    • \(S_2\)：\(t_2\) 时刻出售现货资产时的即期价格
    • \(F_2\)：\(t_2\) 时刻平仓期货头寸时的期货价格
    • \(b_2 = S_2 - F_2\)：\(t_2\) 时刻平仓时的基差
  • 实际有效销售价格 (Effective Selling Price)：
    \[ \text{实际有效销售价格} = S_2 + (F_1 - F_2) = F_1 + (S_2 - F_2) = F_1 + b_2 \]
  • 在空头套期保值中，若基差走强 / 扩大 (Strengthens)（即 \(b_2\) 增大），套保者将因此获益。

Short Hedge and Basis

  • Suppose a short hedge is initiated at time \(t_1\) and closed out at time \(t_2\):
    • \(F_1\): Futures price when hedge entered at \(t_1\)
    • \(S_2\): Spot price when asset sold at \(t_2\)
    • \(F_2\): Futures price when hedge closed out at \(t_2\)
    • \(b_2 = S_2 - F_2\): Basis at \(t_2\)
  • Effective Price Received:
    \[ \text{Effective Selling Price} = S_2 + (F_1 - F_2) = F_1 + (S_2 - F_2) = F_1 + b_2 \]
  • In a short hedge, the hedger benefits if the basis strengthens (\(b_2\) increases).

多头套期保值

  • 多头套期保值 (Long Hedge) 是指在期货市场上建立期货多头（买入）头寸。
  • 适用于企业已知未来必须采购某种资产、并希望提前锁定采购成本的经营情境。
  • 案例分析： 某电气制造厂商已知 5 月份需要采购 100,000 磅铜。1 月份时，5 月到期铜期货价格为 $3.20/磅。
    • 该制造商买入 4 张 5 月铜期货合约（每张 25,000 磅）。
    • 5 月份时，在实体即期市场上以价格 \(S_2\) 买入铜现货，同时以价格 \(F_2\) 平仓期货多头。

Long Hedge

  • A long hedge involves taking a long position in futures contracts.
  • Appropriate when a company knows it will have to purchase an asset in the future and wants to lock in the purchase price.
  • Example: An electrical manufacturer knows it will need 100,000 lbs of copper in May. In January, May copper futures trade at $3.20/lb.
    • Manufacturer buys 4 May copper futures contracts (25,000 lbs each).
    • In May, buys copper in physical spot market at \(S_2\) and closes out futures at \(F_2\).

多头套期保值与基差

  • 对于在 \(t_1\) 建立、在 \(t_2\) 平仓的多头套期保值：
    \[ \text{实际有效采购价格} = S_2 - (F_2 - F_1) = F_1 + (S_2 - F_2) = F_1 + b_2 \]
  • 请注意，无论对于多头还是空头套期保值，其实际有效实现价格均满足统一公式： \[ \text{实际有效价格} = F_1 + b_2 \]

    • 在空头套保中，保值者希望卖出价格越高越好 → 基差走强（\(b_2\) 增大）有利于空头保值者。
    • 在多头套保中，保值者希望采购成本越低越好 → 基差走弱（\(b_2\) 减小）有利于多头保值者。

Long Hedge and Basis

  • For a long hedge initiated at \(t_1\) and closed at \(t_2\):
    \[ \text{Effective Purchasing Price} = S_2 - (F_2 - F_1) = F_1 + (S_2 - F_2) = F_1 + b_2 \]
  • Notice that in both long and short hedges, the effective price is: \[ \text{Effective Price} = F_1 + b_2 \]
  • Comparison:
    • In a short hedge, hedger wants higher selling price → benefits if basis strengthens (increases).
    • In a long hedge, hedger wants lower purchase cost → benefits if basis weakens (decreases).

套保者平衡：商业诉求与基差结构分解

现货生产商（农户） vs 现货采购商（麦片企业）：

  • 玉米种植户（空头套保者）： 秋收时供给集中爆发。当地天量供给压低现货价格。提前做空期货以锁定生存保障收益；若现货崩盘，期货利润足以弥补。
  • 麦片制造企业（多头套保者）： 鉴于昂贵仓储成本，无法提前储存一整年用粮。通过做多期货锁定原料成本预算，防范农产品价格通胀冲击。

基差风险结构分解：

\[ \text{实际有效价格} = F_1 + \underbrace{(S_2 - F_2)}_{\text{期限错配风险 (Timing Mismatch)}} + \underbrace{(S_2^* - S_2)}_{\text{标的资产错配风险 (Asset Mismatch)}} \]

当对冲平仓日期早于期货到期月份时产生期限错配（\(t_2 < T\)）；当被对冲资产（如航油）与期货标的（取暖油）不同时产生资产错配。

Hedger Balance: Commercial Objectives & Basis Signs

Producer (Farmer) vs Consumer (Cereal Maker):

  • Corn Farmer (Short Hedger): Harvests all supply at once. Massive local supply depresses harvest prices. Sells futures early to guarantee survival revenue. If spot collapses, futures gain compensates.
  • Cereal Company (Long Hedger): Cannot store a full year's grain upfront due to storage costs. Longs futures to lock in raw ingredient budget and protect consumers from food price inflation.

Decomposition of Basis Risk (Chalkboard Derivation):

\[ \text{Effective Price} = F_1 + \underbrace{(S_2 - F_2)}_{\text{Timing Mismatch}} + \underbrace{(S_2^* - S_2)}_{\text{Asset Mismatch}} \]

Timing mismatch occurs when hedge is closed before contract maturity (\(t_2 < T\)); asset mismatch occurs when hedging a product (e.g. jet fuel) using a related contract (heating oil).

基差风险

  • 套期保值策略能够极大地降低风险，但在现实中极少能够实现完全消除风险。这一残存的风险就是基差风险 (Basis Risk)。
  • 基差风险源于在 \(t_1\) 建立对冲时，未来的平仓基差 \(b_2\) 具有不确定性。
  • 基差风险的产生来源：
    • 期限错配 (Maturity Mismatch)： 现货实际买卖日期与期货合约规定的交割月份不完全一致。
    • 资产错配 (交叉对冲 Cross-Hedging)： 需要对冲的现货资产与期货合约的标的资产存在品质或属性差异。
    • 地点错配 (Location Mismatch)： 现货交收地点与期货合约规定的交割地理区域不同。

Basis Risk

  • Hedging strategies minimize risk, but in practice rarely eliminate it completely. This remaining risk is basis risk.
  • Basis risk arises because the basis \(b_2\) is uncertain when the hedge is initiated at \(t_1\).
  • Sources of Basis Risk:
    • Maturity mismatch: The date the asset is bought/sold differs from the futures delivery month.
    • Asset mismatch (Cross-hedging): The asset being hedged is different from the asset underlying the futures contract.
    • Location mismatch: Delivery specifications are at a different geographical point.

最小化基差风险 – 合约选择准则

  • 交割月份的选择准则：
    • 选择交割月份尽可能接近、但必须晚于套期保值结束日期的期货合约。
    • 尽量避免将期货合约持有至实际交割月份：进入交割月后交易流动性往往迅速枯竭，且容易因实物交割物流机制产生剧烈的价格异动。
  • 标的资产的选择准则：
    • 优先选择其期货价格与被对冲现货资产价格相关系数最高的期货合约。

Minimizing Basis Risk – Contract Choice

  • Choice of Delivery Month:
    • Choose a delivery month that is as close as possible to, but later than, the expiration of the hedge.
    • Avoid holding futures into the actual delivery month: trading can become illiquid and erratic price spikes occur due to delivery logistics.
  • Choice of Underlying Asset:
    • Choose the contract whose futures price is most highly correlated with the price of the asset being hedged.

最小方差套期保值比率

  • 套期保值比率 (Hedge Ratio) \(h\) 是指持有的期货头寸规模与面临的现货风险敞口规模之比。
  • 为使被对冲组合资产价值的方差最小化：
    \[ h^* = \rho \frac{\sigma_S}{\sigma_F} \]
  • 公式参数说明：
    • \(\sigma_S\)：在对冲期间现货价格变动量 \(\Delta S\) 的标准差
    • \(\sigma_F\)：在对冲期间期货价格变动量 \(\Delta F\) 的标准差
    • \(\rho\)：\(\Delta S\) 与 \(\Delta F\) 之间的线性相关系数
  • \(h^*\) 在统计学上恰好等于以 \(\Delta S\) 对 \(\Delta F\) 进行普通最小二乘法 (OLS) 线性回归方程的最佳拟合斜率项。

Minimum Variance Hedge Ratio

  • The hedge ratio \(h\) is the size of the position taken in futures contracts relative to the size of the exposure.
  • To minimize the variance of the hedged position value:
    \[ h^* = \rho \frac{\sigma_S}{\sigma_F} \]
  • where:
    • \(\sigma_S\): standard deviation of \(\Delta S\) (change in spot price over hedge horizon)
    • \(\sigma_F\): standard deviation of \(\Delta F\) (change in futures price over hedge horizon)
    • \(\rho\): coefficient of correlation between \(\Delta S\) and \(\Delta F\)
  • \(h^*\) is the slope of the best-fit OLS linear regression of \(\Delta S\) on \(\Delta F\).

交叉对冲规模测算与尾随调整

跨品种对冲案例（航油与取暖油期货）：
某航空公司需采购 2,000,000 加仑航空煤油 (\(Q_A\))。取暖油单张期货合约规模 \(Q_F = 42,000\) 加仑。
已知统计参数：\(\sigma_S = 0.0263\)，\(\sigma_F = 0.0313\)，\(\rho = 0.928\)。

\[ h^* = \rho \frac{\sigma_S}{\sigma_F} = 0.928 \times \frac{0.0263}{0.0313} = \mathbf{0.7796} \] \[ N^*_{\text{未尾随}} = 0.7796 \times \frac{2,000,000}{42,000} = 37.12 \approx \mathbf{37 \text{ 张合约}} \]

尾随对冲调整 (Tailing the Hedge)： 由于期货每日结算而非到期一次性结算，每日现金流可按短期利率 \(r\) 滚动再投资。尾随调整将现货实物数量替换为现值规模 \(V_A / V_F\)，略微调低所需合约数量（例如从 37 张微调至 36 张）。

Cross-Hedge Sizing & Tailing Adjustments

Classic Problem (Airline Jet Fuel hedged with Heating Oil Futures):
Airline needs 2,000,000 gallons of jet fuel (\(Q_A\)). Heating oil contract size \(Q_F = 42,000\) gallons.
\(\sigma_S = 0.0263\), \(\sigma_F = 0.0313\), \(\rho = 0.928\).

\[ h^* = \rho \frac{\sigma_S}{\sigma_F} = 0.928 \times \frac{0.0263}{0.0313} = \mathbf{0.7796} \] \[ N^*_{\text{untailed}} = 0.7796 \times \frac{2,000,000}{42,000} = 37.12 \approx \mathbf{37 \text{ contracts}} \]

Tailing the Hedge (Daily Settlement Impact): Because futures are settled every day rather than at maturity, gains/losses can be reinvested at interest rate \(r\). Tailing replaces physical quantities with present values \(V_A / V_F\), slightly reducing contract count (e.g. from 37 to 36).

Hedging an Equity Portfolio with Index Futures

  • Unsystematic (firm-specific) risk is eliminated by diversification.
  • Systematic (market) risk cannot be diversified away, but it can be hedged using stock index futures!
  • The Capital Asset Pricing Model (CAPM) relates the return on the portfolio to the return on the market:
    \[ R_P - R_F = \beta (R_M - R_F) \]
  • To eliminate market risk (reduce \(\beta\) to 0):
    \[ N^* = \beta \frac{P}{F} = \beta \frac{V_P}{V_F} \]
    where \(P\) is current portfolio market value and \(F\) is index futures contract value.

滚动对冲策略 (逐期展期 Stack and Roll)

  • 面临难题： 套期保值者面临长周期的风险敞口（例如 2 年），但市场上流动性充裕的期货合约通常集中在较短期限（例如 3 到 6 个月）。
  • 应对策略：
    • 在近期活跃合约上建立初始对冲头寸。
    • 在临近合约到期日之前，对冲平仓原有合约，并在下一期活跃合约上建立新的对冲头寸（向前展期滚动）。
  • 展期基差风险 (Roll-over Basis Risk)：
    • 保值者在每次合约展期平仓和新开仓时，都会承担新的基差波动风险。
    • 最著名的历史案例为 Metallgesellschaft (MG，1993年)：该公司在原油市场处于长期升水 (Contango) 结构下，对巨额长达10年的能源供货合同实施短期期货滚动对冲，展期损失导致数十亿美元灾难性亏损。

Rolling the Hedge Forward (Stack and Roll)

  • The Problem: A hedger has a long-dated exposure (e.g., 2 years), but futures contracts are only liquid for short maturities (e.g., 3 to 6 months).
  • The Strategy:
    • Take a hedge position in short-dated contracts.
    • Just before maturity, close out existing contracts and open a new position in the next contract (rolling forward).
  • Roll-over Basis Risk:
    • Each time a contract is rolled over, the hedger incurs basis risk at that rollover point.
    • Made famous by Metallgesellschaft (1993), which suffered massive losses on rolling forward short oil hedges during market contango.
