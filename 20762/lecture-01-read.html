衍生品的本质

  • 衍生品 (Derivative) 是一种金融工具，其价值取决于（或衍生自）另一种更基础的资产的价值。这种资产叫基础资产 (Underlying Asset)，教材里也常叫“标的资产”。
  • 四大基础衍生工具模块：
    • 期货合约 (Futures Contracts)： 在交易所集中交易的标准化协议，约定在未来特定日期以特定价格买入或卖出基础资产。
    • 远期合约 (Forward Contracts)： 在场外市场 (OTC) 私下达成的双边定制协议，约定在未来买入或卖出基础资产。
    • 互换合约 (Swaps)： 双方私下达成的协议，约定在未来多个日期之间周期性互换现金流。
    • 期权合约 (Options)： 赋予买方在未来特定日期或之前以约定价格买入（看涨期权）或卖出（看跌期权）基础资产的权利（而非义务）的合约。
Page 4 · Nature of DerivativesSlide ↗

The Nature of Derivatives

  • A derivative is a financial instrument whose value depends on (or derives from) the values of other, more basic underlying assets.
  • Four fundamental derivative building blocks:
    • Futures contracts: Standardised exchange-traded agreements to buy/sell at a specified future date and price.
    • Forward contracts: Customised bilateral over-the-counter (OTC) agreements to buy/sell in the future.
    • Swaps: Private agreements between two parties to exchange periodic cash flows over multiple dates.
    • Options: Contracts conferring the right (without obligation) to buy (call) or sell (put) an asset.

场外交易 (OTC) 市场

  • 交易所之外的重要替代渠道
  • 由交易商组成、通过电话和计算机联网的交易网络，交易商无需实际碰面
  • 交易通常发生在金融机构、企业财务主管和基金经理之间
  • 优点
    • 更大的自由度 – 合约条款可根据客户需求量身定制（不受交易所规格限制）
    • 无需缴纳保证金
  • 缺点
    • 信用风险（一方不履约的风险；通常适用 ISDA 主协议）；可通过抵押品（担保）降低
  • 2008 年以来 …
    • 场外市场已纳入监管
    • 标准化的场外产品在互换执行设施 (SEF) 上交易
    • 所有交易必须向中央数据库报告
Page 7 · Derivatives MarketsSlide ↗

Over-the-Counter (OTC) Markets

  • Important alternative to exchanges
  • Telephone and computer-linked network of dealers who do not meet physically
  • Trades are usually between financial institutions, corporate treasurers, and fund managers
  • Advantages
    • More freedom – contract terms are tailored to the needs of the client (not exchange specific)
    • No need for margin payments
  • Disadvantages
    • Credit risk (the risk that one of the parties will not honor the contract; documented under ISDA Master Agreements); this can be reduced by collateralization
  • Since 2008 …
    • OTC markets have become regulated
    • Standardized OTC products trade on swap execution facilities (SEFs)
    • All trades must be reported to a central registry

第 9 页 · 远期合约幻灯片 ↗

远期合约

远期合约是一种场外 (OTC) 协议，约定在未来某一时间 T 按某一价格 K（远期价格）买入或卖出某种资产。

  • 同意买入的一方 A 持有多头头寸 (Long Position)。
  • 同意卖出的一方 B 持有空头头寸 (Short Position)。
  • 订立远期合约在初始时刻不需要任何前期现金流支出（f0=0）。
Page 9 · Forward ContractsSlide ↗

Forward Contracts

A forward contract is an OTC agreement to buy or sell an asset at a certain time T in the future for a certain price K – the forward price.

  • The party A that has agreed to buy has a long position.
  • The party B that has agreed to sell has a short position.
  • Entering a forward contract requires zero upfront cash flow (f0=0).
第 10 页 · 期货合约幻灯片 ↗

期货合约

期货合约是一种协议，约定在未来某一时间 T 按某一价格（期货价格 Ft）买入或卖出某种资产。

  • 期货价格在今天观察到，但对应的是将在未来进行的交易
    • 期货价格由供求关系决定，与现货价格的决定方式相同
  • 期货合约在交易所交易
    • 交易所规定交割日 T，并规定以交割基础资产（商品期货）或现金结算（金融期货）的方式了结
    • 每日结算：逐日盯市 (Marking-to-Market)
    • 结算所通过合约更替 (Novation) 保证双方履约（成为每一位买方的卖方，以及每一位卖方的买方）
  • 绝大多数期货合约在到期前被平仓，而不是实物交割。平仓是指进行一笔对冲（反向）交易
    • 并由此产生现金盈亏
Page 10 · Futures ContractsSlide ↗

Futures Contracts

A futures contract is an agreement to buy or sell an asset at a certain time T in the future for a certain price – the futures price Ft.

  • Futures prices are observed today but refer to transactions to be carried out in the future
    • Futures price is determined by supply and demand, in the same way as the spot price
  • Futures contracts are traded on exchanges
    • The exchange specifies delivery date T and settlement by delivery of the underlying (commodity futures) or by cash settlement (financial futures)
    • Are settled daily: marking-to-market
    • The clearinghouse guarantees both sides by novation (buyer to every seller, seller to every buyer)
  • Most futures contracts are closed out before maturity, rather than settled by physical delivery. Closing out a futures position involves entering into an offsetting trade
    • resulting in a cash profit or loss

核心机制差异：远期合约 vs 期货合约

比较维度 远期合约 (Forward Contract) 期货合约 (Futures Contract)
交易场所 场外交易市场 OTC (私下双边协商) 规范化交易所 (如 CME、ASX、ICE)
合约条款 根据用户个性化需求量身定制 高度标准化 (规模、交割品、日期)
结算方式 到期日 T 一次性结算 每日结算 (逐日盯市 Mark to Market)
信用风险 存在显著的交易对手违约风险 由结算所清算担保，基本被消除
提前平仓 较为困难；需征得原对手方同意 在流动性充裕的市场上随时对冲平仓

核心机制总结： 期货利用结算所保证金制度替代了个体交易对手信用风险。由于每日损益都在当日现金结算结清，违约敞口无法像双边远期协议那样跨越数月累积扩大。

Core Structural Differences: Forwards vs Futures

Dimension Forward Contract Futures Contract
Trading Arena Over-The-Counter (private bilateral) Organised Exchange (CME, ASX, ICE)
Contract Terms Customised to user needs Standardised (size, delivery, date)
Settlement Single settlement at maturity T Marked to market daily

Futures replace individual counterparty risk with clearinghouse margin capital. Because gains and losses are realized daily, default exposure cannot accumulate across months as it does in bilateral forward agreements.

期货价格的演变

随着时间推移，市场参与者持续交易在 T 时刻买入/卖出资产的期货合约。

  • 现货价格 St 是资产在 t 时刻立即交割的价格
  • 期货价格 Ft 是在 t 时刻订立、于 T 时刻交割资产的期货合约所约定的交割价格

注意 F0=K

  • 由于期货合约的卖方可以在到期时交割实物资产，期货价格与现货价格在到期时必须相等（FT=ST）。否则，套利者可以获得无风险利润：
    • FT>ST：买入现货、做空期货并交割，利润为 FT−ST
    • FT<ST：卖空资产、买入期货并接受交割，利润为 ST−FT
Page 12 · Futures ContractsSlide ↗

Evolution of Futures Price

As time goes by, market participants continue trading futures contracts to buy/sell the asset at T.

  • The spot price St is the asset price at time t for immediate delivery
  • The futures price Ft is the agreed delivery price for a futures contract initiated at t, for asset delivery at T

Note that F0=K

  • Since the seller of the futures contract can deliver the physical asset at maturity, the futures price and the spot price must be the same at that time (FT=ST). If not, arbitrageurs could make risk-free profits:
    • FT>ST: buy the asset spot, short futures, deliver; profit FT−ST
    • FT<ST: short sell the asset, buy futures, take delivery; profit ST−FT
第 13 页 · 期货合约幻灯片 ↗

保证金制度与要求

  • 保证金是投资者存入其经纪人账户的现金或可流通证券；它是履约担保 (Performance Bond)，而非贷款或购货首付款
    • 初始保证金 (Initial Margin)：开仓时存入
    • 维持保证金 (Maintenance Margin)：触发追加保证金通知前账户的最低余额（通常约为初始保证金的 75%–80%）
    • 追加保证金通知（变动保证金，Margin Call）：余额跌破维持保证金时，投资者必须存入资金，将余额恢复至初始保证金水平
  • 保证金账户余额随每日结算（逐日盯市）而调整
  • 保证金账户可以获得利息
  • 保证金通过减少违约时的损失来控制信用风险
  • 结算所（SFE 结算公司，Clearinghouse）
    • 经纪商在结算所持有结算保证金账户
    • 对所有会员的头寸进行净额结算，并记录当天的全部交易
Page 13 · Futures ContractsSlide ↗

Margin Requirements

  • A margin is cash or marketable securities deposited by an investor with his/her broker; it is a performance bond, not a loan or down payment
    • Initial margin: deposited when the position is opened
    • Maintenance margin: the minimum balance before a margin call (typically about 75–80% of initial margin)
    • Margin call (variation margin): if the balance falls below maintenance margin, the investor must deposit funds to restore it to the initial margin
  • The balance in the margin account is adjusted to reflect daily settlement (marking-to-market)
  • Interest is earned on margin accounts
  • Margins control credit risk, by reducing the loss in the event of default
  • Clearinghouse (SFE Clearing Corporation)
    • Broker holds a clearing margin account with the Clearinghouse
    • Netting positions of all members and keep track of all transactions in one day

E-Mini 标普500 追加保证金逐步计算案例

参数设定： 交易者 A 以 2,800 点买入多头 1 张 E-Mini S&P 500 期货合约。合约乘数 = $50/指数点。名义价值 = 2,800×$50=$140,000。
初始保证金 = $5,500 • 维持保证金 = $5,000 • 账户初始资金 = $5,500。

  • 第 1 天： 指数下跌 5 点。当日损失 = 5×$50=$250。账户余额降至 $5,500−$250=$5,250（高于 $5,000 维持线 → 无需追加保证金）。
  • 第 2 天： 指数继续下跌 10 点。新增损失 = 10×$50=$500。账户余额降至：
    账户余额=$5,250−$500=$4,750<$5,000 ⟹触发追加保证金通知 (Margin Call)!
  • 应追加变动保证金额度：
    追加保证金=$5,500−$4,750=$750

重要区分： 投资者必须注入 $750 将账户余额恢复至 初始保证金 $5,500，而绝不能只追加 $250 恢复到维持保证金！

Interpreting the Gold Forward Curve & Market Expectations

ETQ 2 Problem Setup: Gold futures on COMEX (2 Feb 2009): Feb $907.00, Apr $909.50, Jun $912.40, Aug $915.20, Oct $918.00, Dec $921.10.

    • The curve is strictly upward-sloping (FFeb<FApr<⋯<FDec) → Contango.
    • For an investment asset like gold, the slope reflects the cost of carry (r+u): interest rate to finance gold storage plus vault/insurance costs.

期权合约导论

  • 期权是一种合约，赋予持有人在某一日期之前（美式）或在某一日期当天（欧式）(T)，按某一价格 (K) 买入/卖出基础资产的权利（而非义务）
    • 买方为获得这一权利，向卖方（立权人 Writer）支付期权费（权利金）
    • 欧式：仅能在到期日行权；美式：到期日及之前任何时间均可行权

卖方（空头，立权人）卖出的义务买入的义务

  • An option is a contract which confers a right (not an obligation) to buy/sell an underlying asset by (American) or at (European) a certain date (T) for a certain price (K)
    • The buyer pays the seller (writer) a premium for this right
    • European: exercised only on the expiration date; American: at any time up to and including it

金融衍生品的核心用途

  • 套期保值 (Hedging)
    • 针对已经拥有或计划买卖的资产，防范价格发生不利变动；降低风险、锁定价格

  • 改变负债的性质（例如互换）

    • 做市商、共同基金、对冲基金

Uses of Derivatives

  • Hedging
    • Seek protection against unfavourable price changes in an asset already owned or planned to be bought/sold; reduce risk and lock in the price
  • Speculation

    • Eliminates market price discrepancies
  • To change the nature of a liability (e.g. swaps)

期货投机与期权投机对比

  • 期货投机特征：
    • 前期资本投入极小（仅需缴纳履约保证金）。
    • 线性损益结构：潜在收益无上限，但潜在亏损同样无底线，且可能远远超过最初存入的保证金本金。
  • 期权投机特征：
    • 前期最大损失严格限定于已支付的期权费权利金。
    • 非线性损益结构：潜在收益无上限；下行亏损有限，最大仅为 100% 权利金；上行杠杆在股价大幅突破时可带来数倍爆发性回报（>300%）。
    • 但前提是股价必须大幅超越执行价格，足以抵消购买期权所支付的时间价值。
Page 28 · SpeculationSlide ↗

Speculating with Futures and with Options

  • Futures Speculation:
    • Small upfront investment (only margin deposit required).
    • Linear payoff: Potential gain is unlimited, but potential loss is also unlimited and can exceed initial margin.
  • Options Speculation:
    • Upfront cost is strictly limited to the option premium paid.
    • Non-linear payoff: Potential gain is unlimited, while downside loss is strictly capped at 100% of premium (limited loss); upside leverage can deliver explosive returns (>300%).
    • However, requires stock to move significantly beyond the strike price to overcome the time value paid.

黄金的理论期货价格

  • 期货价格应该是多少？
  • 若黄金现货价格为 S，一份 T 年后交割的期货合约的期货价格为 F，则
    F=S(1+r)T
    其中 r 为 1 年期（本币）单利利率。
  • 在我们的例子中，S=$1,390，T=1，r=0.05，因此 F=$1,390(1+0.05)=$1,459.50。
  • 若 F>S(1+r)T：套利者借款买入现货并做空期货（正向现货-期货套利 Cash-and-Carry）。
  • 若 F<S(1+r)T：套利者卖空现货、贷出所得资金并买入期货（反向现货-期货套利 Reverse Cash-and-Carry）。
  • 若采用连续复利（Hull 记号），同一关系写作 F0=S0erT。

The Futures Price of Gold

  • What should the futures price be?
  • If the spot price of gold is S and the futures price for a contract deliverable in T years is F, then
    F=S(1+r)T
    where r is the 1-year (domestic currency) simple interest rate.
  • In our examples, S=$1,390, T=1 and r=0.05, so that F=$1,390(1+0.05)=$1,459.50.
  • If F>S(1+r)T: arbitrageurs borrow, buy spot, short futures (cash-and-carry).
  • If F<S(1+r)T: arbitrageurs short spot, lend the proceeds, buy futures (reverse cash-and-carry).
  • With continuous compounding (Hull's notation) the same relation reads F0=S0erT.

案例分析 – 原油期货套利 (消费性资产)

  • 参数设定： 原油即期现货价格 S=$85，利率 r=5% p.a.，年化仓储成本 u=2% p.a.。

    • 此时是否存在无风险套利机会？不存在！

  • Setup: Spot oil S=$85, r=5% p.a., storage cost u=2% p.a.
