衍生品的本质

  • 衍生品 (Derivative) 是一种金融工具，其价值取决于（或衍生自）其他更为基础的标的资产 (Underlying Assets) 的价值。
  • 四大基础衍生工具模块：
    • 期货合约 (Futures Contracts)： 在交易所集中交易的标准化协议，约定在未来特定日期以特定价格买入或卖出标的资产。
    • 远期合约 (Forward Contracts)： 在场外市场 (OTC) 私下达成的双边定制协议，约定在未来买入或卖出标的资产。
    • 互换合约 (Swaps)： 双方私下达成的协议，约定在未来多个日期之间周期性互换现金流。
    • 期权合约 (Options)： 赋予买方在未来特定日期或之前以约定价格买入（看涨期权）或卖出（看跌期权）标的资产的权利（而非义务）的合约。

The Nature of Derivatives

  • A derivative is a financial instrument whose value depends on (or derives from) the values of other, more basic underlying assets.
  • Four fundamental derivative building blocks:
    • Futures contracts: Standardised exchange-traded agreements to buy/sell at a specified future date and price.
    • Forward contracts: Customised bilateral over-the-counter (OTC) agreements to buy/sell in the future.
    • Swaps: Private agreements between two parties to exchange periodic cash flows over multiple dates.
    • Options: Contracts conferring the right (without obligation) to buy (call) or sell (put) an asset.

场外交易 (OTC) 市场

  • 场外交易市场是场内公开组织化交易所的重要替代与补充。
  • 由金融机构、基金管理公司和大型企业财务主管通过电话和计算机网络全球互联构成的交易网络。

Over-the-Counter (OTC) Markets

  • Important alternative to public organised exchanges.
  • Telephone and computer-linked global network of financial institutions, fund managers, and corporate treasurers.
  • Trades are negotiated directly between two commercial parties (bilateral contracts).
  • Terms can be tailored precisely to the client's risk management requirements (custom notional, expiry, delivery specs).
  • Subject to counterparty credit risk (default risk) – governed largely by ISDA (International Swaps and Derivatives Association) Master Agreements.

远期合约

  • 远期合约是一种在场外市场 (OTC) 达成的协议，约定在未来特定时刻 \(T\) 按预先商定的交割价格 \(K\) 买入或卖出某种资产。
  • 交易一方持有多头头寸 (Long Position)（同意在时刻 \(T\) 以价格 \(K\) 买入资产）。
  • 另一方持有空头头寸 (Short Position)（同意在时刻 \(T\) 以价格 \(K\) 卖出资产）。
  • 在到期时刻 \(T\) 的损益公式（其中 \(S_T\) 为到期标的资产即期价格）：
    \[ \text{多头到期损益} = S_T - K \] \[ \text{空头到期损益} = K - S_T \]
  • 订立远期合约在初始时刻不需要任何前期现金流支出（初始价值 \(f_0 = 0\)）。

Forward Contracts

  • A forward contract is an OTC agreement to buy or sell an asset at a certain future time \(T\) for a specified delivery price \(K\).
  • One party assumes a long position (agrees to buy the asset at \(T\) for \(K\)).
  • The other party assumes a short position (agrees to sell the asset at \(T\) for \(K\)).
  • Payoff at maturity \(T\) where \(S_T\) is the spot price:
    \[ \text{Payoff to Long Position} = S_T - K \] \[ \text{Payoff to Short Position} = K - S_T \]
  • Entering a forward contract requires zero upfront cash flow (\(f_0 = 0\)).

期货合约

  • 期货合约是一种在未来特定时间 \(T\) 按特定价格（期货价格 \(F_t\)）买入或卖出某种资产的协议。
  • 与远期合约不同，期货合约在有组织的公开交易所内集中竞价交易：
    • 标准化规格： 标的资产质量等级、合约乘数/规模、交割地点、交割月份均由交易所统一标准化设定。
    • 结算所 (Clearinghouse)： 通过合约更替机制 (Novation) 保证双方履约（成为每一位买方的卖方，以及每一位卖方的买方）。
    • 保证金制度 (Margin Requirements)： 实行每日无负债结算（逐日盯市 Marking-to-Market），有效化解违约信用风险。
  • 绝大多数期货合约在到期日之前都会通过对冲平仓 (Closed Out) 结束，极少数最终走向实物交割。

Futures Contracts

  • A futures contract is an agreement to buy or sell an asset at a certain time \(T\) in the future for a certain price (futures price \(F_t\)).
  • Unlike forwards, futures contracts are traded on organised exchanges:
    • Standardised specifications: Exact underlying grade, contract size, delivery location, delivery months.
    • Clearinghouse: Guarantees performance of both sides via novation (becomes buyer to every seller, seller to every buyer).
    • Margin requirements: Daily settlement (marking-to-market) mitigates default risk.
  • Most futures contracts are closed out prior to maturity rather than settled by physical delivery.

核心机制差异：远期合约 vs 期货合约

比较维度 远期合约 (Forward Contract) 期货合约 (Futures Contract)
交易场所 场外交易市场 OTC (私下双边协商) 规范化交易所 (如 CME、ASX、ICE)
合约条款 根据用户个性化需求量身定制 高度标准化 (规模、交割品、日期)
结算方式 到期日 \(T\) 一次性结算 每日结算 (逐日盯市 Mark to Market)
信用风险 存在显著的交易对手违约风险 由结算所清算担保，基本被消除
提前平仓 较为困难；需征得原对手方同意 在流动性充裕的市场上随时对冲平仓

核心机制总结： 期货利用结算所保证金制度替代了个体交易对手信用风险。由于每日损益都在当日现金结算结清，违约敞口无法像双边远期协议那样跨越数月累积扩大。

Core Structural Differences: Forwards vs Futures

Dimension Forward Contract Futures Contract
Trading Arena Over-The-Counter (private bilateral) Organised Exchange (CME, ASX, ICE)
Contract Terms Customised to user needs Standardised (size, delivery, date)
Settlement Single settlement at maturity \(T\) Marked to market daily
Credit Risk Significant counterparty risk Virtually eliminated by Clearinghouse
Closing Out Difficult; requires counterparty consent Immediate offset in liquid market

Futures replace individual counterparty risk with clearinghouse margin capital. Because gains and losses are realized daily, default exposure cannot accumulate across months as it does in bilateral forward agreements.

期货价格的收敛与演变

  • 随着时间推移，市场参与者持续对在到期时刻 \(T\) 买卖资产的期货合约进行撮合交易。
  • 期货价格 \(F_t\) 根据标的供求、即期现货价格、利率以及仓储成本/股息收益的变化持续动态更新。
  • 到期价格收敛性 (Price Convergence at Maturity)：
    • 当合约临近交割月份（\(t \to T\)）时，期货价格必然向现货即期价格收敛：
      \[ F_T = S_T \]
    • 若 \(F_T > S_T\)：套利者可买入现货、做空卖出期货，并立即进行交割，锁定确定性无风险利润 \(F_T - S_T\)。
    • 若 \(F_T < S_T\)：持有现货的套利者可卖空现货、买入多头期货，并接受交割，获取无风险利润。

Evolution of Futures Price

  • As time goes by, market participants continue trading futures contracts to buy/sell the asset at \(T\).
  • The futures price \(F_t\) continuously updates based on supply, demand, spot prices, interest rates, and storage/dividends.
  • Price Convergence at Maturity:
    • As delivery month is reached (\(t \to T\)), the futures price must converge to the spot price:
      \[ F_T = S_T \]
    • If \(F_T > S_T\): Arbitrageurs buy spot, short futures, make delivery for guaranteed profit \(F_T - S_T\).
    • If \(F_T < S_T\): Arbitrageurs holding asset short spot, buy futures, take delivery.

Margin Requirements

  • A margin is cash or marketable securities deposited by an investor with his/her broker:
    • Serves as a performance bond (collateral), not a loan or down payment.
    • Protects the broker and clearing house against possible default.
  • Initial Margin: The amount that must be deposited in the margin account when the position is first opened.
  • Maintenance Margin: The minimum level to which the balance in the margin account may fall without triggering a margin call (typically ~75% to 80% of initial margin).
  • Variation Margin / Margin Call: If balance drops below maintenance margin, the investor receives a call requiring funds to restore the balance back to the initial margin level.

    • 反向现货-期货套利失效：因为普通市场参与者极难在现货市场直接借入并卖空实物原油；而商业库存持有者由于需要维持生产运营的便利收益 (Convenience Yield)，也不愿清空出售现货库存。

Example – Oil Futures Arbitrage (Consumption Asset)

  • Setup: Spot oil \( S = \$85 \), \( r = 5\% \) p.a., storage cost \( u = 2\% \) p.a.
  • Scenario 1: Quoted 1-year futures \( F = \$93 \):
    • Cost of carry upper bound: \( F_{\text{max}} = 85 \times (1 + 0.05 + 0.02) = \mathbf{\$90.95} \).
    • Since \( \$93 > \$90.95 \), cash-and-carry arbitrage WORKS (borrow, buy oil, store, sell futures → guaranteed profit).
  • Scenario 2: Quoted 1-year futures \( F = \$82 \):
    • Is there an arbitrage opportunity? NO!
    • Reverse cash-and-carry fails because market participants cannot easily short sell physical crude oil, and commercial inventory holders will not sell stock due to operational convenience yield.
