案例分析 – 无套利分析原理

  • 考虑由以下资产构成的投资组合：
    • 持有 \( \Delta \) 份标的资产多头，以及
    • 卖出 1 份看涨期权空头
  • 到期时投资组合的价值为 \( (\Delta \t\times S_T) - f_T \)：
    • 若股票上涨，组合价值为 \( (\Delta \t\times \$22) - \$1 \)
    • 若股票下跌，组合价值为 \( (\Delta \t\times \$18) - \$0 \)
  • 当两种状态下的到期支付完全相等时，该投资组合即为无风险组合：
    \[ (\Delta \t\times \$22) - \$1 = (\Delta \t\times \$18) - \$0 \]
    由此可解得：
    \[ \Delta = \frac{\$1 - \$0}{\$22 - \$18} = \mathbf{0.25} \]
  • \( \Delta \) 代表期权价格变动与股票价格变动的比率（对冲比率）。

Example – No-Arbitrage Arguments

  • Consider the portfolio consisting of:
    • a long position in \( \Delta \) units of the asset, AND
    • a short position in one call option
  • The value of portfolio at maturity is \( (\Delta \t\times S_T) - f_T \):
    • If the stock moves up this is \( (\Delta \t\times \$22) - \$1 \)
    • If the stock moves down it is \( (\Delta \t\times \$18) - \$0 \)
  • The portfolio is riskless when the payouts in both states are equal:
    \[ (\Delta \t\times \$22) - \$1 = (\Delta \t\times \$18) - \$0 \]
    which implies that:
    \[ \Delta = \frac{\$1 - \$0}{\$22 - \$18} = \mathbf{0.25} \]
  • \( \Delta \) represents the change in the price of the option divided by the change in the stock price.

  • The terminal value of the portfolio is \( uS_0\Delta - f_u = dS_0\Delta - f_d \).

“风险中性”概率

  • 风险中性投资者认为所有资产价格均以无风险利率 \( r \) 增长，因此在对未来所有现金流估值时均以 \( r \) 进行折现。
  • 在“风险中性”世界中：
    • 所有资产的预期收益率都等于无风险利率 \( r \)
    • 资产的期初现值等于其到期预期回报以无风险利率贴现后的数值
  • 股票上涨的“风险中性”概率 \( p \) 满足：
    \[ E[S_T] = S_0 e^{rT} \implies S_0 = e^{-rT} E[S_T] = e^{-rT} [p S_u + (1 - p) S_d] \]
  • 真实世界（实际）中股票上涨的概率 \( p_a \) 与风险中性概率 \( p \) 存在本质差异。
    • 若资产的实际预期收益率为 \( \mu \)，则： \[ S_0 = e^{-\mu T} E_a[S_T] = e^{-\mu T} [p_a S_u + (1 - p_a) S_d] \]

The “Risk-Neutral” Probability

  • Risk-neutral investors believe that asset prices grow at the risk-free rate \( r \) and thus value all future cash-flows by discounting at \( r \).
  • In a “risk-neutral” world:
    • the expected return of every asset is the risk-free rate \( r \)
    • the initial value of an asset is equal to its expected payoff at maturity, discounted at the risk-free rate
  • The “risk-neutral” probability \( p \) of an up movement satisfies:
    \[ E[S_T] = S_0 e^{rT} \implies S_0 = e^{-rT} E[S_T] = e^{-rT} [p S_u + (1 - p) S_d] \]
  • The real-world (actual) probability \( p_a \) of stock going up is different to the risk-neutral probability \( p \).
    • When \( \mu \) is the expected return on the asset, then: \[ S_0 = e^{-\mu T} E_a[S_T] = e^{-\mu T} [p_a S_u + (1 - p_a) S_d] \]

Risk-Neutral Valuation in General

  • Let \( p \) be the “risk-neutral” probability of an up movement: \[ S_u = u S_0 \, (p), \qquad S_d = d S_0 \, (1 - p) \]
  • In a “risk-neutral” world, the initial value of an asset is equal to its discounted expected payoff at maturity, discounted at \( r \):
    \[ E[S_T] = S_0 e^{rT} \implies p S_u + (1 - p) S_d = S_0 e^{rT} \quad (3) \] \[ E[f_T] = f_0 e^{rT} \implies p f_u + (1 - p) f_d = f_0 e^{rT} \quad (4) \]
  • From (3) we solve for \( p \):
    \[ p u S_0 + (1 - p) d S_0 = S_0 e^{rT} \implies p = \frac{e^{rT} - d}{u - d} \]
  • From (4) the general pricing formula is:
    \[ f_0 = e^{-rT} [p f_u + (1 - p) f_d] \]

风险中性定价 – 连续红利收益率

  • 在“风险中性”世界中，支付连续复利红利收益率 \( q \) 的股票预期收益率为 \( r - q \)。因此：
    \[ E[S_T] = S_0 e^{(r - q)T} \implies p S_u + (1 - p) S_d = S_0 e^{(r - q)T} \quad (5) \] \[ E[f_T] = f_0 e^{rT} \implies p f_u + (1 - p) f_d = f_0 e^{rT} \quad (6) \]

    \[ p u S_0 + (1 - p) d S_0 = S_0 e^{(r - q)T} \implies p = \frac{e^{(r - q)T} - d}{u - d} \]
  • 由方程 (6) 将预期到期回报按无风险利率 \( r \) 折现：
    \[ f_0 = e^{-rT} [p f_u + (1 - p) f_d] \]

Risk-Neutral Valuation – Dividend Yield

  • In a “risk-neutral” world, the expected return on a stock paying a continuously compounded dividend yield \( q \) is \( r - q \). Thus:
    \[ E[S_T] = S_0 e^{(r - q)T} \implies p S_u + (1 - p) S_d = S_0 e^{(r - q)T} \quad (5) \] \[ E[f_T] = f_0 e^{rT} \implies p f_u + (1 - p) f_d = f_0 e^{rT} \quad (6) \]

    \[ p u S_0 + (1 - p) d S_0 = S_0 e^{(r - q)T} \implies p = \frac{e^{(r - q)T} - d}{u - d} \]
  • From (6) we discount the expected payoff at \( r \):
    \[ f_0 = e^{-rT} [p f_u + (1 - p) f_d] \]

连续红利收益率 \( q \) 下的股指看涨期权定价

问题：某股票指数当前点位为300点。在未来3个月内（\( T = 0.25 \)），指数预期上涨或下跌10%（\( u = 1.10, \, d = 0.90 \)）。年化无风险利率 \( r = 8\% \)，连续红利收益率 \( q = 3\% \)。对执行价格 \( K = 300 \) 的3个月期欧式看涨期权进行估值。

步骤 1：考虑持有成本调整后的概率 \( p \)

在风险中性世界中，标的指数以 \( r - q = 8\% - 3\% = 5\% \) 的净增长率增长：

\[ p = \frac{e^{(0.08 - 0.03) \t\times 0.25} - 0.90}{1.10 - 0.90} \] \[ = \frac{1.01258 - 0.90}{0.20} = \mathbf{0.5629} \] \[ 1 - p = 0.4371 \]

注意此时 \( p = 56.29\% \) 低于不分红资产的上涨概率（\( 60.10\% \)），因为股息派发稀释了资本利得增长。

步骤 2：期权到期回报与估值计算

到期指数点位及看涨期权回报：

  • 上涨：\( S_u = 330 \implies c_u = \mathbf{30} \)
  • 下跌：\( S_d = 270 \implies c_d = \mathbf{0} \)

以无风险利率 \( r \) 折现：

\[ c_0 = e^{-rT} [p c_u + (1 - p) c_d] \] \[ = e^{-0.08 \t\times 0.25} [0.5629 \t\times 30 + 0.4371 \t\times 0] \] \[ = e^{-0.02} \t\times 16.8868 = \mathbf{16.55 \text{ 指数点}} \]
贴现时使用 \( e^{-rT} \) 而非 \( e^{-(r-q)T} \)，因为期权持有者仅享有到期时指数点位的结算收益，而在期权存续期内并不享有现金股利分红！

Index Call Option Valuation with Continuous Dividend Yield \( q \)

Question: A share index currently trades at 300 points. Over the next 3 months (\( T = 0.25 \)), it is expected to increase or decrease by 10% (\( u = 1.10, \, d = 0.90 \)). Risk-free rate \( r = 8\% \) p.a., dividend yield \( q = 3\% \) p.a. (continuous). Value a 3-month European call with strike \( K = 300 \).

Step 1: Carry-Adjusted Probability \( p \)

In a risk-neutral world, the underlying grows at rate \( r - q = 8\% - 3\% = 5\% \):

\[ p = \frac{e^{(0.08 - 0.03) \t\times 0.25} - 0.90}{1.10 - 0.90} \] \[ = \frac{1.01258 - 0.90}{0.20} = \mathbf{0.5629} \] \[ 1 - p = 0.4371 \]

Notice \( p = 56.29\% \) is lower than for a non-dividend asset (\( 60.10\% \)) because dividends drain capital growth.

Step 2: Payoffs & Valuation

Terminal index levels and call payoffs:

  • Up: \( S_u = 330 \implies c_u = \mathbf{30} \)
  • Down: \( S_d = 270 \implies c_d = \mathbf{0} \)

Discount back at risk-free rate \( r \):

\[ c_0 = e^{-rT} [p c_u + (1 - p) c_d] \] \[ = e^{-0.08 \t\times 0.25} [0.5629 \t\times 30 + 0.4371 \t\times 0] \] \[ = e^{-0.02} \t\times 16.8868 = \mathbf{16.55 \text{ index points}} \]
Critical Insight from Video: We discount at \( e^{-rT} \), NOT \( e^{-(r-q)T} \), because the option holder receives the final index payoff but does NOT receive the cash dividend yield during the life of the option!
